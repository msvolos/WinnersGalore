The core read was right. Freddie B had run 91, 90 and 88 at this exact class and matched the par, and the earnings and connections screens backed him up. The write-up called the scratch correctly and had Rugged Love as the flyer. Bourbon Flight's flagged risks (the class jump and post 11 in a two-turn route) showed up. The one real miss was C McGriff, dismissed with the slower group, who ran 2nd at a big price.

Takeaway

This is the method's best case: a top pick whose figures already sit at par for the class, at a fair price, with live connections. Picks built on a pace setup have struggled. Picks built on figures that are already good enough have been the ones to cash.

Playable horses, ranked

Freddie B (5-1) is the top pick. Per start is about $13,000 this year on 3 starts and $24,000 off his lone 2025 start (a maiden win). The trend is steady at a high level: Beyers 91, 90, 88 in three tries at exactly this N1X class against the tougher New York crowd, including a game 2nd at Saratoga at a mile (2p inside, kept on) and a 3rd in a 91. That 91 matches today's par, and he has run it twice. His TimeformUS late pace is 95, and this race has real early speed (Wonder Mist 113 early, Find No Fault 106, Rugged Love 103), so a stalker/closer should get a race set up for him. Connections are hot at the meet: Esquivel 20% (4 for 20) and McPeek 21% (4 for 19), and the pair are 18% with a $2.19 ROI at Keeneland. The last Belmont line (6th, 4-path turn, no rally) is the one blemish; I give it a partial pass as a wide trip. The risk: first try at 1 1/16, and he has not won since his maiden, so he can find a way to run 2nd or 3rd.

Bourbon Flight (7-2) is the second choice, right on the line. Per start about $26,200 on 7 starts this year (up from $2,000 last year) with four wins, and he is 2 for 3 with a 2nd at today's distance. His best dirt figure is a 94 winning a mile allowance at Ellis (beating Rugged Love, who was 7th), the fastest dirt figure in the playable group, and he followed it with a rallying 2nd on turf. Jose Ortiz is 23% on the year, though Joe Sharp is only 11% at the meet. Flags: he jumps from a $50k starter allowance to the open N1X, he breaks from post 11 in a two-turn route, and at 7-2 he can easily drift off your list.

Rugged Love (8-1) is the flyer. Per start about $27,000 on 4 starts (two wins, one by DQ), Beyers 75, 88 (the DQ win), 83, then 85 winning at this exact 1 1/16 trip at Churchill last out (bid 3 wide, dueled, left the lead). Gaffalione 22% and Walsh 20% at the meet, 19% together at Keeneland with a $2.23 ROI. He needs a forward trip, and the 7th behind Bourbon Flight at Ellis shows what happens when he gets into a speed fight; with Wonder Mist and Find No Fault in here, that is the worry.

Wonder Mist (6-1). Per start about $24,100 this year, padded by a $50k 2nd in the St. Louis Derby (83 Beyer, dueled outside, game). He is fast early (113 early pace, best in the field) and has hot connections (Irad Ortiz 24%, Wesley Ward 27% at the meet), but his route figures (75, 83) are well below par and his best numbers came sprinting. Likely to be part of a pace duel.

Fuzzbuster (6-1). Highest per start ($70,000 in 2026) but that is turf stakes money; his only dirt win was a 90 on a sloppy track. Oliver is 7% at the meet. Cross-entered Friday, so likely gone.

Find No Fault (20-1). Per start about $19,100; Beyers 91, 89, 85, 82, 85, a flat to falling line after a hot spring. J/T 4% together. Speed that probably cooks with Wonder Mist.

Bridgesong (20-1), C McGriff (20-1), Slobberknocker (30-1), Sure to Appeal (30-1). Top figures of 78, 80 (last out 56), 75, and 66. None has run fast enough for this class, and none shows a rising trend that would justify the price.

Bottom line

Freddie B at 5-1 is the play: the most consistent par-level dirt figures among the playable horses, a closing style that suits a speed-heavy race, and the meet's hot jockey and trainer. Bourbon Flight at 7-2 is the second choice if he stays on your list at post time, and Rugged Love at 8-1 is a small saver for the distance-proven horse with hot connections. Watch for a Fuzzbuster scratch, which helps your prices.

Bottom line

Never Really Know at 8-1 is the play: proven at the mile and the course, rising turf figures, fair barn. Cinquante Cinq is the second choice and the one to key with her, provided she holds 7-2 or better on the board. Sudden Rise is a small flyer at 8-1 for the late kick. Watch the board for Collective Beauty getting hammered, which only helps the prices on the others, and for the Ring Rights and Screamin Nina scratches.

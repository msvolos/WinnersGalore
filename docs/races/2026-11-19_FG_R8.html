The contenders

The key race is the Sep 19 LA Cup Juvenile at Louisiana Downs: Star of Bethlehem won it by 4 1/2 with a 72, Pastinaca was 3rd (54) and Levee Native 7th (43). The other link is the Aug 1 Yong Futurity at Evangeline, won by Golfito (not entered) over Star of Bethlehem and Hes Steelin Mylady.

Yo' Lion (8-1). The best figure in the race by a lot. An 85 winning a Louisiana Downs maiden by 6 1/2 on Sep 23, on the lead and cruising home, and his 1:04.15 for 5 1/2 furlongs was nearly a second faster than Star of Bethlehem's stakes win at the same track and distance four days earlier. His only other start was a 17 in the LA Legacy on Oct 31, 7 furlongs on a muddy track, brief speed and done as the 2.10-1 favorite. I throw that line out: wrong surface condition and a longer trip for a quick 2-year-old. He was gelded after it and worked 5 furlongs in 1:02.3 here on Nov 11. Nieves wins 13%, Foster 12%, and the pair are 15% together the last two years ($2.09 ROI), though 0-for-7 at Fair Grounds and Foster is 0-for-11 in stakes. The risk is that the 85 was a one-off and the 17 is the real horse, and he has to deal with Essential George and Lucky Spot for the lead. About 14% to win.

Star of Bethlehem (7-2). The proven stakes winner. Figures climb 52, 58, 72, and the 72 came wiring the LA Cup Juvenile on the rail and kicking clear. He was 2nd, beaten a half length, in the Yong Futurity before that after dueling. 2-for-4, $20,900 a start. He moved to David Terre (23% this year, 33% first time with a new trainer), Pedroza wins 14%, and the J/T is 1-for-7. Early 106, so he will be in the speed fight with Essential George, Lucky Spot and Yo' Lion. Off since Sep 19, with a bullet 3-furlong breeze here on Nov 12. About 24% to win.

Essential George (3-1). One start, a 67 winning a 5-furlong maiden at Delta Downs by 3 1/2, on the lead and clear. The fastest early speed in the race (Early 118). Landry wins 26%, Hernandez 15%, and the pair are 3-for-9 (33%). He is the lightly raced improver with a hot barn, but he is stretching out to 6 furlongs and facing winners in a stakes for the first time off a 67. The likely favorite, and the morning line already prices him short. About 22% to win.

Hes Steelin Mylady (9-2). The most seasoned horse here, with three thirds in tougher company: a 74 in a Keeneland OC 100k N1X at 1 1/16 on a good track (held on for show), a 68 in the open Sapling Stakes at Monmouth (dueled, tight late), and a 55 in the Yong Futurity after breaking in and getting bumped. He won his Indiana debut with a 55. Back to a sprint and down to state-bred company, both positives. West wins 23%, Murrill 14%, the J/T is 22% (46 starts, $1.77), and West is 27% going from routes to sprints. Only 1-for-4 and his best fast-track figure is 68. About 20% to win.

Bottom line

Yo' Lion is the top pick and the only overlay on the board (value ratio 1.26). He is a bet at 8-1 or better; anything shorter and the second choice takes over. Star of Bethlehem is the second choice: Hes Steelin Mylady rates a hair higher on value (1.10 vs 1.08), so the close call goes to Star of Bethlehem on win chance (24% vs 20%), and he becomes a bet at 9-2 or better. Hes Steelin Mylady needs 11-2. Essential George is the horse to beat and an underlay at 3-1. Touch Money is the flyer at 12-1.

The main risk to the top pick is that the 85 was a one-race wonder. He has run once well and once terribly, and with four horses showing early speed he may not get the easy lead he had at Louisiana Downs.

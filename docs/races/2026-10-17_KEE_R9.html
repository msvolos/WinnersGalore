Grade 2 Raven Run Stakes, 7 furlongs on dirt, 3-year-old fillies, $400,000. Twelve horses, no scratches listed. Handicapped on 2026-10-17 from the DRF Formulator page (full field, 12/12 shown). Odds are morning line; check the board at post time (5:16 PM ET).

Off the list on the odds rule

Mashallah (8-5) is the class of the race on paper: 4-for-5, a 106 Beyer at Keeneland in her maiden win, and a 92 when drawing off in the Prioress (G3) last out. She is too short to play. She is also a pure speed horse (TimeformUS early pace 112) drawn next to Movie Night (early 114), so the front end should be contested.

No probable scratches. Everyone's last start is three-plus weeks back.

Pace shape

Mashallah and Movie Night are the two fastest early horses by a wide margin, with Mythical (99) and With a Kiss (103) also inclined to press. That sets up a stalker or closer. The best late numbers belong to Percy's Bar (111), Hen Party (96), Star Actress (93), Conceit (89) and Yo Fancy (89).

Playable horses, ranked

Conceit (4-1), the pick. Per-start of about $97,200 this year on four starts (three wins), up from $15,300 as a 2-year-old, so she has improved sharply. The trend is as solid as it gets: Beyers of 94, 94 and 94 in three straight wins, the last two at this exact 7 furlongs (Audubon Oaks and the Dogwood G3, where she ran down Explora and Movie Night). She is 3-for-3 on fast dirt and 2-for-2 at the distance. Connections are the best in the field: Irad Ortiz Jr. (24% this year, 20% at the meet) and Brad Cox (25%), and the pair wins 29% at Keeneland with a $1.58 ROI. She sits off the pace (early 82, late 89), which is exactly where you want to be with Mashallah and Movie Night likely to duel. The risk: she has not run a figure close to Mashallah's 106, and 4-1 is near your floor, so if the crowd pounds her below 7-2 she falls off the list.

Percy's Bar (6-1), second choice. Highest per-start in the race both years (about $118,500 this year and $111,200 last year), and she is a Keeneland specialist: 3 starts here, 2 wins (including the Ashland G1) and a second. Throw out the Music City on turf (60 Beyer, shuffled, wrong surface). Her last dirt race was a big one, an 86 when second in the Test (G1) at 7 furlongs after stumbling at the start. She has the best late-pace number in the field (111), and this pace should suit her. Joel Ortiz is 23% this year, but trainer Ben Colebrook is only 10%, and she carries 123 pounds, five more than most of the field. Her figures (77 to 87) sit a clear notch below Conceit's.

Movie Night (9-2). Per-start about $39,300. Beyers 83, 84, 96, then a throwout 59 in the Test (stumbled at the break), then a gutsy 94 when third in the Dogwood after dueling. Flavien Prat is 38% at the meet and Walden is 25%. Talented, but she wants the lead and Mashallah wants it more. A pace casualty unless Mashallah stumbles.

A Fine Chardonnay (12-1). Per-start about $57,700 this year, $81,400 last year. Unbeaten at Keeneland (2-for-2, including the Beaumont G2 at this distance). Beyers 86, 74, 85, 93, then 81 in the Dogwood, so the trend has turned down from that 93. Ian Wilkes wins 30% in graded stakes with a $5.11 ROI, a real longshot angle, but jockey Hernandez is only 6% at the meet.

Mythical (12-1). Big earner as a 2-year-old ($90,100 per start) but only $31,900 this year, and the figures have slid from 94s in the spring to 70, 82 (turf) and 81. Going the wrong way.

With a Kiss (20-1), flyer. 2-for-2, per-start $66,000 this year. Beyers 70 then 86 in an allowance win off a 10-month layoff, so the second start back could bring more. Pletcher and Velazquez win 40% together at Keeneland ($3.08 ROI). Big class jump and likely to be caught in the speed duel, but the price is right for a small saver.

Hen Party (20-1). Per-start $38,200. Mostly a turf and synthetic filly (86 top on synthetic); her one fast-dirt start was a 78 when third to Mashallah. The J/T combo is 3-for-7 with a $5.22 ROI, which is interesting, but the dirt figure is not.

Star Actress (30-1). Per-start $23,700. Router cutting back to 7 furlongs, figures in the 70s and 80s (86 top). Will close but probably not enough.

Army's Marauder (30-1), Yo Fancy (30-1), Coco Connect (50-1). Figures of 78, 77 and 74 at best. Army's Marauder and Yo Fancy are maiden or allowance types jumping into a G2; Coco Connect has been well beaten in graded company.

Bottom line

Conceit at 4-1 is the play: three straight 94s, proven at the trip, the best jockey and trainer in the race, and a stalking style that fits a race with two speedballs up front. Percy's Bar at 6-1 is the second choice and the horse to switch to if Conceit drifts under 7-2 on the board. With a Kiss at 20-1 is a small flyer on the hot Pletcher and Velazquez combo.

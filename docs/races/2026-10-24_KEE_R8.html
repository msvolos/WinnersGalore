What happened

Willy D's went to the front in the three to two path, then moved to the inside with So Sandy pressing him and Tumbarumba close up. A :48.61 half and 1:12.87 for six furlongs was honest but not the speed duel the write-up expected. Stars and Stripes and So Sandy collared him turning for home and Stars and Stripes put a head in front in upper stretch, but Willy D's fought back along the inside and got the lead back late at about 8-1.

San Siro never got a chance. He brushed with a rival soon after the start, then bumped and steadied hard in a jam on the first turn. He swung out to the five path in upper stretch and ran into traffic at the furlong pole, finishing 8th at about 26-1. Tiztastic trailed, swung six wide and rallied to 5th. Tumbarumba stalked and weakened to last, and favorite Nevada Beach went seven wide and made little impact.

Grading the handicap

Ranking Willy D's second paid off, and the case for him held: proven at the trip, Maker and Velazquez in good form, and the best per-start money among the playable horses. The pace call was wrong. The write-up saw a crowded early fight that would set it up for San Siro, but Gearing Up scratched, Willy D's controlled it, and only Tiztastic closed with any kick. San Siro's trouble was real, so the result says little about him. Stars and Stripes, dismissed at 3-1, went off at about 4-1 and would have qualified at post time.

Takeaway

Second straight race where the speed held and the projected pace collapse did not happen. When one need-the-lead horse scratches, rethink the pace before trusting a closer to get the setup.

Playable horses, ranked

Risk: she carries 123 to the 3-year-olds' 118, she is 0-for-2 on CD turf (61 top), and if someone goes with her early the stamina edge could disappear. Price and pace are the case.

2. Charm of Venice (GB) (4-1). The improver with the strongest barn. Irish maiden winner going about 1 3/8 ("led final 110 yards, won going away"), then 66 in the KD Oaks (G3) off a stumble at the start, then 79 in the Keeneland N1X after being bumped at the start and swinging seven wide into the lane ("willing"). Risk: she needs pace to run at, and this race may not have any.

3. Onehotmess (7-2). Right on the line and likely to get bet below it. She drew off by a big margin winning a 1 3/8-mile Woodbine maiden on good ground Oct 4 (79, a career best), and she is 2-for-2 in the money at this distance. Beyers 72, 66, 72, 79 are rising. The question is the jump from maidens to a $127K N1X in her first start for these connections at CD, and she gives five pounds to the 3-year-olds.

4. Heart Spin (15-1). A 6-year-old mare who is 1-for-23, but CD turf is her course (8 1 1 1, $113,197) and she has hit the board twice in three tries at this distance (79). Ran a 72 rallying from six wide for 2nd in a $50K claimer here Sep 17, and she ran an 80 in this N1X condition last November. Navarrete is 12%; the pair are 15% at CD with a $4.32 ROI. Flyer only.

5. Ring Rights (30-1). Best figure 68, recent 62 and 59, trained by a 13% barn that is 0-for-3 on the dirt-to-turf angle. Toss.

Bottom line

Marcinkowski at 9-2 is the play: the top figure in the race, the only proven marathoner, and the one horse who should control the pace. Charm of Venice at 4-1 is the second choice and the closer to use with her, since she has the excuse last time and the better barn. Onehotmess is a fair third but will probably go off below 7-2. Heart Spin at 15-1 is a small flyer on her CD turf record. If Marcinkowski drops under 7-2 on the board, pass her and move to Charm of Venice.

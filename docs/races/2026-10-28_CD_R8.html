What happened

Initialize broke 2nd and led narrowly while vying with Ortley Avenue on his outside into and around the turn through :21.72 and :44.32. He shook free in upper stretch and held sway through midstretch, but got collared in the final sixteenth and was nailed at the wire. He went off at 2.90-1, so he would not have qualified at post time. Ortley Avenue weakened between horses in upper stretch and finished last at 3.05-1.

Twilight Delight stalked two wide, tipped three wide into the lane, made gradual progress to bid at the sixteenth pole and wrestled the lead away at the wire at about 5.60-1. Ganaas tracked from the two path and needed more late. Utopian broke last of 12, trailed along the inside, shifted to the four path in upper stretch and never factored at 29.90-1. Initialize was claimed out of the race.

Grading the handicap

The pace read was right: Initialize and Ortley Avenue hooked up early, and the fractions were quick. Initialize was good enough to survive the duel and kill off Ortley Avenue, which is a solid read on him as the class of the speed, but the stalker behind them got the last jump. The write-up ranked Twilight Delight 6th, calling his 84 well short of par, and he beat the top-figure horse anyway. It also flagged that Initialize could go under 7-2, and he did. The Sprint Out Pass scratch call held. Utopian had no early speed in a race that needed position and was never close.

Takeaway

The speed held most of the way this time and the top pick paid on the place and show end. A tight duel in a short turf sprint left the door open for a stalker sitting just off it, so a lightly raced improver right behind the speed deserves more credit than its figure alone suggests.

Playable horses, ranked

1. Initialize (7-2). Best figures in the field and the best connections. Per-start is $16.6k this year on eight starts (4 wins), up from $14.5k last year. His last three Beyers are 91, 87, 91, the 91 last out coming at this exact OC 80k/N2X level at Kentucky Downs, where he set the pace inside and was caught on the wire by a neck. The risk is the price and the pace: he and Ortley Avenue both want the lead, and he is 7-2 on the line with the kind of connections that get bet, so he may not qualify at post.

A real flyer if he handles the grass.

The rest (Moon Sniper with figures falling 90, 80, 65; Bal a Kazoo, winless this year; Sprint Out Pass, a dirt horse entered elsewhere) are tosses. Among the also-eligibles, B D Valeski and The Minkster would be minor factors if they drew in.

Bottom line

Initialize at 7-2 is the top pick. He has the top figure (91, one off par), the speed, and a 29% J/T at Churchill, and the last four recaps have all been won by the speed or the top-figure horse. Utopian at 10-1 is the second choice and the value side of the ticket: he already beat N1X company over this exact course and trip and is still improving. If Initialize drops below 7-2 at post, Utopian is the play. Corona de Oro at 15-1 is the flyer for the first-time turf angle and the bullet works.

  • Good Vintage (5-1), second pick: scratched. Refunded ($0.00).

What happened

The write-up flagged Cozzy Trickstress (early 100) and Pwr of Persistence as the horses who would fight My Bestie for the lead. Cozzy Trickstress ducked out at the start but still made the front, set the pace along the two path and took the rail into the lane. Pwr of Persistence was forced out at the start, saved ground and never got involved. My Bestie did not go for the lead; she tracked along the three path, chased the leader through a :46.30 half, responded in the stretch and got up in the final stride to share the win as Cozzy Trickstress drifted out late. Redzone Gal sat four wide the whole way and finished with interest for 3rd. Financial Star weakened after a ground-saving trip into the turn.

Grading the handicap

The top pick was right: My Bestie was the best horse, coming off 48 days and still catching a loose leader. The pace read was half right. The speed went to Cozzy Trickstress, and with My Bestie rating behind her instead of dueling, she got a comfortable lead that nearly held. The write-up called Cozzy Trickstress a 4-1 underlay at 10%, and she was the one that held on. The price call was right too: the crowd hammered My Bestie to 7-10, so the dead heat paid almost nothing.

Takeaway

A short-priced top pick can win and still lose value, and a dead heat cuts the price even more. This is the second time in three days (Bodacious Bay at Belmont) that a top pick went off well under the floor, so the post-time odds rule should keep sorting these out.

Bottom line

Neither pick is an overlay on the morning line. Isola d'Oro has the best value ratio (1.00) and Hey Pal is close behind (0.92); with the two within 0.1, the close-call rule takes the higher win chance, so Hey Pal is the top pick and Isola d'Oro the second choice.

  • Hey Pal: a bet at 9-2 or better (value ratio 1.25 at 4.4-1).
  • Isola d'Oro: a bet at 11-2 or better (1.25 at 5.25-1).
  • Flyer: Inonit at 10-1 or more, on the July 76 and the 44% barn.

What can go wrong with Hey Pal: he has been caught late twice this year after making the lead, and he will have Fess and Start the Day for company early.

At the window: if Hey Pal is shorter than 9-2 and Isola d'Oro is 11-2 or longer, play Isola d'Oro. If either takes a lot of money (shorter than its morning line), that has been a good sign in the tracked races; a big drift is a warning.

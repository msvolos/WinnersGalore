What happened

The write-up called Stronsino the lone speed with Cool Intentions stalking him. Instead the two of them went at it from the start. Stronsino was coaxed from the gate and dueled from the two path, with Cool Intentions four then three wide right alongside. Yo Daddy was hustled away and sat five then four wide in closest aim of the pair. The fractions were moderate for nine furlongs on slop, so the duel did not cook either one.

Cool Intentions came under coaxing at the five-sixteenths, took narrow command just outside the quarter pole and went seven wide into the lane. Yo Daddy came eight wide to his outside and the two fought to the wire, with Cool Intentions gamely holding him off by a head. Classicist was detached last early, tucked inside on the turn and kicked on late for 3rd. Stronsino swung four to five wide into the stretch, lost the lead and weakened to 5th. Tuscan Sky chased wide, went nine wide into the lane and came up empty.

Grading the handicap

The top pick read was right. The rising figure line (85, 86, 88) kept climbing, and the stakes placing at the distance last out translated to a win against the class of the field. The write-up named the class gap as his risk and Yo Daddy as the horse to beat; that is exactly who he had to beat, and he did. Stronsino's slop form did not save him once he had company early. Classicist, ranked 4th as the proven distance horse, got the show. Tuscan Sky, flagged for the eased-and-walked line, ran last.

Takeaway

A lightly raced improver at a price beat two short-priced favorites, which is the exact horse the method is built to find. Both picks drifted up at post, so the bets easily cleared the value floor.

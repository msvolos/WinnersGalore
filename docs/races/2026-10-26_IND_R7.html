What happened

Annie Express went to the front and was clear early, leading through :46.90 and 1:11.25. She held the lead into the stretch, then gave way near the sixteenth pole and faded to 6th. She was hammered at the windows to about 2.40-1, well under the 7-2 line, so the top pick would not have qualified at post time.

Collected Bond stalked the pace on the inside and was steadied in tight quarters near the eighth pole. She angled out to the four path, rallied, and got there in time under a mild drive at about 3.80-1, still a playable price. Lady Blake stalked three wide, bid at the eighth pole and was clear late before getting caught. Ranch Life stalked in the four path and offered a mild late bid for 3rd. I Made It, the 5-2 morning line favorite, stumbled at the start and had no late bid.

Grading the handicap

The scratch call was right: both main-track-only horses came out. The write-up tossed the Oct 1 duel for both Annie Express and Collected Bond, and only one of them bounced back. Annie Express got a clear early lead at reasonable fractions and still could not finish a mile, so the "faded when the pace was hot" risk understated the problem. Collected Bond's rising figures and her ability to rate were the right read, and she won from a stalking trip even after trouble. Ranch Life, ranked 4th for her rising line, filled the trifecta.

Takeaway

When the top pick takes heavy late money down below the minimum, the rule would have kept us off her. Pairing the course horse with the improver paid off here, and the Top 2 ticket turned a loss into a $7.20 profit.

Playable horses, ranked

1. Annie Express (10-1), top pick. Best per-start among the playable horses in both years ($8.0k and $8.5k), and the course horse: 11-5-1-1 on the Indiana Grand turf for $136k. Her figures sit closest to par of anyone playable who is sound: 71 winning an N2X allowance here in June, 67, 66, a 63 in the Peony H. ($100k) and a 67 on Sep 16 finishing 3rd at this exact OC15k level, ahead of Ranch Life, Lady Blake and Ziggzappa. I am throwing out the Oct 1 line (28 Beyer at 1.10-1): it came at 7 1/2 furlongs, short of her best trip, in a head-to-head duel with Collected Bond that cooked them both. Back at a mile, dropping out of stakes and OC30k company. Connections are fair: Rechy is 13% with a strong turf stat (21%, $3.87 ROI), Sanjur 12% picks up the mount. The risk is the last-out flop: if that 28 means something is wrong, she is a throw-out, and she has faded when the early pace was hot.

2. Collected Bond (5-1), second choice. The improver. Three-for-four this year with a $7.4k per-start on a small sample, the kind of number worth a look. Beyers climbed 46, 58, 63, the last two wins at a mile and 1 1/16 on this turf ("3p, rally" and "duel, bid, mild drive"), so she can rate or go. I am excusing the 55 on Oct 1 for the same reason as Annie Express: the shorter trip and the duel. Davis is 13% with a 14% turf stat ($2.85 ROI); Moncada is 11% and the J/T is weak (8%). The catch is class and figures: up from $12.5k claiming, and her 63 top is 13 points under par.

3. Ziggzappa (20-1), flyer. The distance specialist: 18-9-2-1 at a mile on turf and 20-9-2-1 on turf overall, with figures up to 79. She is 7 and fading (64, 63, 62, then a 45 at this level last out with "no factor"), so this is a connections bet: Grubbs is 27% at the meet and 37% on turf ($4.51 ROI), and Jimenez is 24% this year. Best late kick in the field if the speed does come back.

4. Ranch Life (8-1). The only one in the race with a clean rising line: 56, 57, 61, 62, the 62 a 3rd behind I Made It on Oct 1 after being impeded. She was a closing 4th at 72-1 at this level on Sep 16. Concepcion is 18% at the meet. But per-start is low, her top is 14 under par, and the trainer is 9% on turf. Win money only if you buy the pace.

5. Ce La Vi Charli (6-1). On paper the class of the playable horses: won an OC20k at a mile on this turf in July with a 75 (par level), 8-3-0-0 at the distance, and Garcia/Mendoza are 24% together at the meet ($2.64). But she was pulled up and vanned off on Aug 19, has two trainer scratches since, and is now entered for a $10k tag. A drop like that off an injury is a warning sign. Pass at 6-1.

6. Lady Blake (10-1). Won a 1-mile turf claimer here in August with a 67, then a 56 and 5th at this level. Seven-pound apprentice and a 2-for-4 J/T sample help, but Meeks is 8% and she is another speed horse in a crowded front end.

7. Airo Dinero (8-1). Steady 55 to 64 shipper with a vet scratch on Oct 6 and a trainer who is 0-for-4 at the meet. Pass.

The rest (Ziva, White Lilacs, Lucky Shore, Cause Ima Rose) lack the figures or the barn. White Lilacs's trainer is 0-for-36 this year.

Bottom line

Annie Express at 10-1 is the play: best per-start, a proven course horse and figures near par, with the last line tossed. Collected Bond at 5-1 is the second choice for the Top 2 ticket. Ziggzappa at 20-1 is a small flyer on the Grubbs turf angle. Watch the board: if Annie Express is pounded below 7-2 she is off the list, and the MTO scratches should push the other prices up a bit.

What happened

Sister Troienne went to the front along the inside with Faye's Gold pressing outside her, and nobody made her work. A :48.99 half and 1:13.07 for six furlongs is a soft pace for a Grade 2 turf mile. She shook clear entering the lane, shifted in and switched back to her left lead in midstretch, and still held sway at about even money.

Define stalked along the rail in third the whole way, got the two path into the lane, moved out to the four to five path in midstretch and ran on to hold 2nd at about 7-2. Dakotah Blue was a little keen into the first turn, then stalked three wide, floated out to the six path leaving upper stretch and bid for the place late but was denied. Faye's Gold was no match turning for home and faded to 7th. Ultimate Love swung seven wide and moved up for 4th. Essential Lady sat at the rear, fanned seven wide and never factored.

Grading the handicap

A good read on our two horses, and both hit the board. The pace call was wrong: the write-up had Faye's Gold on the lead with the favorite coming from off the pace. Sister Troienne made the lead instead, and the slow fractions handed her the race. Define did exactly what the write-up said she would, sitting just off the pace, and Dakotah Blue's G3 mile form held up from a wide trip. The favorite was off the list on the odds rule, as she should be at 6-5. That's How We Love did not scratch as expected and ran 5th. Essential Lady, ranked third, ran last. Define went off at 3.71-1, just above the 7-2 line, so the bet still qualified at post time.

Takeaway

The method found the two best horses at a price, and the only one better was the 6-5 favorite who got an easy lead. On a soft pace the top pick's show and place money paid for the ticket, which is the WPS structure doing its job.

Pace

Faye's Gold goes to the lead, with Ultimate Love, Define and Essential Lady (early pace 92 to 95) close behind. That is an honest pace, which helps Sister Troienne and Dakotah Blue, who come from a little farther back.

Bottom line

Define at 9-2 is the play: rising figures, a last-out 86 that matches the favorite, and the best rider and trainer combination at the meet. Dakotah Blue at 10-1 is the second choice and the value. She already won a graded mile on turf, and she has a hot new barn. Essential Lady at 8-1 is the third horse to use. Watch for That's How We Love to scratch. If Define drifts under 7-2 on the board, she falls off your list and Dakotah Blue becomes the bet.

Race recap

Result: Verge won ($6.00, $2.80, $2.20), Morrisseau 2nd, Groovinharmony 3rd. A 2-year-old optional claiming $75k / N1X, 6 1/2 furlongs on a fast synthetic track in 1:18.14 (:21.95, :44.73, 1:10.89). The $1 exacta 1-5 paid $10.75 and the 20-cent trifecta 1-5-2 paid $6.53. No scratches.

Our picks

  • Morrisseau (7-2), top pick: 2nd. Returned $5.80 ($3.30 place, $2.50 show) (-$0.20). He went off at 2.25-1, above the 2-1 floor but well under the 18-5 the write-up asked for.
  • Traction Park (9-2), second pick: 6th (last). Returned $0 (-$6.00). She went off at 4.15-1, about half the 8-1 the write-up wanted.
  • Top 2 Picks net for the race: -$6.20.

What happened

The write-up expected Morrisseau and Groovinharmony to be the speed, with Traction Park rating behind them. Instead Traction Park broke inward, was urged to the front and set the pace in the two to three path, with Morrisseau shadowing her after he brushed a foe at the start and had a brief early lead. The half went in :44.73, quick for this group, and the chart's inquiry into the start brought no change.

Morrisseau edged closer late on the turn, took a narrow lead past the quarter pole and could not hold it. Verge had settled off the pace inside, moved out midway on the turn, came four wide into upper stretch, took over and drew off under moderate urging. Morrisseau kept on for 2nd. Groovinharmony tracked two wide, came out five deep and missed the place. Traction Park gave way in the stretch and finished last.

Grading the handicap

The top pick ran to his figure and did not bounce; he simply met a better horse today. Verge was ranked 2nd at 22% and dismissed as an underlay at 2-1, and he won at exactly 2-1, so the write-up's own line said that price was too short, not that he could not win. The pace call was off: the projected speed duel happened, but between Morrisseau and Traction Park, and it set up the inside stalker. The case for Traction Park (rating behind speed off an excused trip) fell apart once she went to the lead.

Takeaway

The steady horse with a rising figure line beat the one big new top. Like Tap to Open at Belmont yesterday, the winner sat just off a contested pace, so the pace read should weigh who benefits from a speed duel, not only who is in it.

Bottom line

Morrisseau is the top pick. He has the only figure that wins this at par for the level, and at 7-2 the value ratio is 1.22, just short of an overlay. Bet him at 18-5 (3.6-1) or better. The worry is the bounce off a big new top while stretching out and fending off Groovinharmony early.

Traction Park is the second choice on the next best value ratio, with the second-best figure and an excuse for her last race. Neither is an overlay on the morning line; she becomes a bet at about 8-1.

Verge is the horse to beat on consistency, and Groovinharmony on connections, but both are underpriced at 2-1 and 5-2. Barger is the flyer at 6-1 or more.

The contenders

Sinister Smile (5-1) is the top pick. His one turf try was an 82 at Kentucky Downs (6 1/2f, swung widest and gaining, 4th beaten about a length), and last out he was 2nd with a 79 in the Keeneland N1X that came off the turf onto a sloppy track. His life best is an 85, and that came winning at a mile. The real edge is the barn: Irad Ortiz Jr. is 37% at the meet, Joe Sharp is 27% at the meet, and together they are 50% at Churchill ($2.84 ROI). The risk is plain: he is 1-for-17 with five seconds, so he has a habit of finding one horse better, and this is his first turf route. About 20%; fair 4-1, and 5-1 gives 1.20.

Irish Law (4-1) is the speed and the likely favorite. Lightly raced 3-year-old with Beyers 56, 71 (wiring a maiden at Ellis), 81 (dueled on the lead and yielded late, beaten about a length in an N1X at 1 1/16m). Throw out the 61 at Kentucky Downs (stepped slow, steadied, cut back to 6 1/2f). Early speed of 113 is the best in the main body, and Atlal (106, but a sprinter) is the only one who figures to hassle him. Gaffalione 15%, Walsh is 0-for-14 at the meet, but the combo is 23% at Churchill (130 starts, $2.36). About 19%; fair 4.3-1, and 4-1 gives 0.95. Underlay, but the most likely winner along with the top pick.

Golden Channel (8-1) is the second choice. Steadiest route figures in the field: 84, 85, 80 on turf in his last four routes, the 85 the best recent turf figure in the main body, and he is 4-1-1-0 at the distance. Throw out the 67 (off-turf slop, steadied) and the 75 (a mile and a half, too far). He is 1-for-12 and keeps getting outkicked, and the connections are thin: Geroux 11%, Weaver 0-for-2 at the meet (16% on the year), and the pair 0-for-2 together, which is too small a sample to hold against him. About 13%; fair 6.7-1, and 8-1 gives 1.17.

City of Oscars (6-1) won a Colonial allowance with an 82 on yielding ground and ran an 80 at Kentucky Downs in his last start, beaten about seven lengths. Concepcion 14%, Casse 20%, and the combo is 22% at Churchill with a big $5.99 ROI. He is 3-for-18, off since Aug 30, and the figures sit a few points below the top three. About 11%; fair 8.1-1, and 6-1 gives 0.77.

The rest

Sheriff Bart (10-1) is the flyer: an 82 running 2nd at Ellis on yielding turf at a mile, and a 78 leading most of the way at this course and distance in May, with the last two (65, 66) in sprints he can be excused for. Alvarado is 9% at the meet, so the connections floor keeps him off the top spots (about 8%). Natural Rights (8-1) is stuck at 75-76 with Arrieta 10% and Cunningham 0-for-4. Without Caution (6-1) and Ready for Peace (15-1) are back from layoffs of 15 and 19 months with figures in the 70s. Epic Horsepower (12-1) topped out at 72, Time for Money (20-1) at 74 with Beschizza 0% at the meet, Atlal (20-1) is a sprinter stretching out, and Taterpie (15-1) runs his figures in sprints and has not shown it around two turns. Nobody in the field has run a Beyer at par (86) on turf this year, so this is a soft, wide-open N1X.

Bottom line

Nobody is an overlay on the morning line. Top pick is Sinister Smile (hot Ortiz/Sharp barn, 82 in his only turf start, 85 life best at a mile); he becomes a bet at 21-4 or better. Second choice is Golden Channel (best recent turf figures, 4-1-1-0 at the distance); he needs 9-1 or better. Irish Law is the horse to beat on speed and becomes the value at 6-1 or longer. Sheriff Bart is a small flyer at 12-1 or more.

Race recap

Result: Epic Horsepower won ($46.26, $23.04, $11.88), Taterpie 2nd ($21.40, $11.16), Sheriff Bart 3rd ($4.20 show). What happened

Golden Channel set the pace on the inside with Taterpie pressing outside him, and the fractions were moderate for a turf mile. The two vied around the far turn, Taterpie took over into the lane, and Golden Channel faded through the final furlong. Sinister Smile came out at the start, saved ground along the rail, then steadied when blocked behind the leaders leaving upper stretch. He split rivals to bid at the furlong grounds, dueled with the outside horses in the final sixteenth and flattened out. Sheriff Bart sat in the pocket, waited for room on the turn, bid four wide and got outkicked late. Epic Horsepower was well back saving ground, swung five wide in the last furlong and caught Taterpie at the wire. Irish Law got into striking range turning for home and then faded to 11th.

Grading the handicap

This one was a miss. The winner was in "The rest," dismissed as topping out at a 72, and the runner-up Taterpie was written off as a sprinter who had not shown it around two turns. The flyer, Sheriff Bart, did run 3rd at 4.61-1, but he was bet well below the 12-1 the write-up wanted. Sinister Smile had a real excuse when he steadied, though he still flattened out late, which fits his 1-for-17 habit. The read on Irish Law as an underlay held.

Takeaway

In a soft N1X where nobody had run at par, the figures did not separate the field, and two horses the method dismissed ran 1-2 at 21-1 and 22-1. When a race is this open, the write-up should lean harder on its "no overlay" call and treat the result as close to random.

The contenders

Sinister Smile (5-1) is the top pick. His life best is an 85, and that came winning at a mile. The real edge is the barn: Irad Ortiz Jr. is 37% at the meet, Joe Sharp is 27% at the meet, and together they are 50% at Churchill ($2.84 ROI). The risk is plain: he is 1-for-17 with five seconds, so he has a habit of finding one horse better, and this is his first turf route. About 20%; fair 4-1, and 5-1 gives 1.20.

Epic Horsepower (12-1) topped out at 72, Time for Money (20-1) at 74 with Beschizza 0% at the meet, Atlal (20-1) is a sprinter stretching out, and Taterpie (15-1) runs his figures in sprints and has not shown it around two turns. Nobody in the field has run a Beyer at par (86) on turf this year, so this is a soft, wide-open N1X.

Bottom line

Nobody is an overlay on the morning line. Top pick is Sinister Smile (hot Ortiz/Sharp barn, 82 in his only turf start, 85 life best at a mile); he becomes a bet at 21-4 or better. Second choice is Golden Channel (best recent turf figures, 4-1-1-0 at the distance); he needs 9-1 or better. Irish Law is the horse to beat on speed and becomes the value at 6-1 or longer. Sheriff Bart is a small flyer at 12-1 or more.

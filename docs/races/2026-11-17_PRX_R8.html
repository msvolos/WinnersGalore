What happened

The pace was honest. The opening quarter in :22.24 and the half in :45.57 are quick for a field where nobody had run to par lately, which fits the write-up's read of Rock Me Gently as the lone speed with West of Lex close behind her. Rock Me Gently finished last, so the lead did not hold up. Without the chart there is no word on positions or trouble, but the order of finish says the speed came back and the pressers and closers took over late. Don't Listen ran 4th and Catch Me a Catch 5th.

Grading the handicap

A solid read with the order flipped. The two picks both hit the board, and the winner was the horse with the best figures in the race, which the write-up ranked second only on a close-call tiebreak (1.04 vs 1.00) that went to Angie's Reward on win chance. The Rock Me Gently call held cleanly: the 5-2 underlay on a 64 ran last. The miss was Coppedge, the 15-1 shot dismissed in The rest as a 51 filly just claimed, who split our picks and blew up the exacta. West of Lex went off about 2.30-1 off her $6.60 win price, above the 2-1 floor but well short of her 11-2 bet price, so the win would not have qualified at post time.

Takeaway

At Finger Lakes the day before, the win-chance tiebreak picked the right horse. Here it picked the wrong one, and the best-figure horse beat the better-connected closer. When the ratios are within a few points, the figure edge deserves more weight than the barn.

The contenders

Nobody in this field has run to par 73 lately. The best recent figures sit in a tight 64 to 71 band, so connections and form direction carry more weight than usual.

Angie's Reward (3-1). The most proven filly at this level and the best barn. She ran a 66 second in an Alw N1X at this exact trip at Parx last out (October 21), far back early and rallying on the rail. Before that a 57 third, then a run of four wins in a row through the summer (64, 69, 61, 61) as she climbed from $10k claimers. She is 6-for-14 lifetime, 5-for-9 at Parx and 3-1-1-1 at 6 1/2. Padilla-Preciado wins 26% at the meet, Gonzalez 14%, and the pair are 41% together at Parx with a $2.79 ROI, the strongest connection number on the card. The risk: she is a closer (TimeformUS Late 75, Early 63) in a race with one clear speed horse, and her 66 is still seven points under par. About 26% to win.

West of Lex (4-1). The best figures in the race. A 71 second in an OC 16k N1X at 7 furlongs in September, a 67 on a Delaware route (inside speed, gave way, a race I discount for the two turns), and a 69 last out in an OC 75k N2L at this trip, where she won a long duel, opened up and got nailed by Hunting Echoes. That is a steady 67 to 71 line from a well-bred 3-year-old (Practical Joke, $135k), and her last race came against a tougher condition. Pennington wins 15% and the J/T is 17% at Parx, but Servis is only 6-for-78 (8%) at the meet. The knock is that she is 1-for-12 and has been second or third in four of her last five without finishing. She will be close to Rock Me Gently early (Early 92). About 20% to win.

Rock Me Gently (5-2). The lone true speed (TimeformUS Early 104, nobody else above 92) and the likely favorite. She won an Alw N1R (Pennsylvania-bred restricted) last out with a 64, a brief duel and drew away by 6. Her other 2026 figures are 56, 54, 44 and 55, with a 79 back in April as the outlier. When she tried this open N1X condition in July at this distance she dueled and gave way, beaten 11. Martinez wins 13%, Guerrero 9%, and the pair 11% together. The lead helps her more than anyone, but a 64 and weak connections make 5-2 too short. About 17% to win.

Don't Listen (7-2). Same barn as Angie's Reward with Silvera (21%), J/T 29% at Parx. Her last three: 68 fourth in a Delaware N1X (stepped slow, rushed up), 66 third at Delaware at 7-5, and 63 third in a Parx N1X at this trip. She has been close at this level without winning it, and she is a 5-year-old who has been 2-for-14 since 2025 began outside of claiming races. She is 3-2-0-1 at the distance. About 16% to win.

Bottom line

Neither pick is an overlay on the morning line. Angie's Reward is the top pick (value ratio 1.04, the best on the board and the highest win chance among the playable horses) and becomes a bet at 4-1 or better. West of Lex is the second choice (1.00, a close call that went to Angie's Reward on win chance) and becomes a bet at 11-2 or better. Rock Me Gently is the horse to beat if she gets loose on the lead, but 5-2 is a big underlay on a 64. Browneyedvalentine is the flyer at 8-1.

The main risk to the top pick is the pace: if Rock Me Gently gets an easy lead, a closer with a 66 may run out of ground.

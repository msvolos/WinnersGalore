What happened

Four scratches turned a nine-horse race into a five-horse race and took out our top pick, Big Blue Line and In Spades. That also thinned the speed. The write-up expected a contested half with Happy Offering, Freedom's Echo, Big Blue Line, Preacha Meyers and Red Zone Runner all wanting to be close. Without the chart there is no record of how the pace actually played out, so this recap does not guess at positions. What is clear: the favorite won, the speedy Red Zone Runner held 2nd, and the closer Waitlist got up for 3rd. Preacha Meyers paid $5.60, so he went off around 1.8-1, shorter than his 3-1 morning line.

Grading the handicap

The write-up named Preacha Meyers the horse to beat and left him off the list only on price, and he won. Waitlist, the flyer on best late pace, hit the board. The miss was Red Zone Runner, ranked near the bottom on figures (best dirt-fast Beyer 73), who ran 2nd. The write-up also said "no probable scratches" and four horses came out, including Dreambuilder. Happy Offering's named risk was finding one too many late after three straight 2nd- or 3rd-place finishes as the favorite. This time he did not even hit the board in a small field.

Takeaway

When a field shrinks to five, the place and show pools get small and the favorite's edge grows. A late-scratched race like this one is a good spot to pass the remaining pick unless it still clears the 3.5-1 line on the board.

Playable horses, ranked

1. Dreambuilder (7-2) has the best recent figure in the field and the clearest upward line: 76, 82, 90. The 82 was a 5 1/4-length win at this exact OC 50k/C level at Parx in August ("stalked, bid, handily"), and the 90 came last out when 2nd by 1 1/2 in a $75k stakes, rallying along the rail. Per-start is $8,621 this year and $10,449 last year, and he is 3-2-0 in 8 starts at Parx. The risk: he is 0-for-4 at 1 mile 70 yards (all before this form turn), and with the top figure he may be bet under 7-2. Pass him if he goes off shorter.

2. Happy Offering (5-1) has the most money per start in the field ($18,608 on 9 starts this year, three wins and four 2nds) and is 3-2-1 in 6 tries at this distance. Figures 87, 85, 79, 81 put him right with Preacha Meyers. Pennington is 16%, Spina 13%. The concerns: he is the fastest horse early and will have company, he is first time at Parx, and he has run 2nd or 3rd three straight times as the favorite, which says he finds one too many late.

3. Waitlist (8-1) is the pace-collapse play. Best late pace in the race (99), won his last start at Parx (77, swung wide and drew clear), and the barn angle is strong: Dunn/Martinez are 33% at Parx ($2.77) and Dunn is 50% with last-out winners ($6.94). Per-start $11,021. The figures (77, 82, 83, 78) are a step below the top two and he moves up from an OC 25k.

4. Big Blue Line (9-2) ran 2nd at this level last out (84) and his line is rising (79, 81, 84), with Sanchez (28%) riding. But he is 0-for-9 this year at seven, and he will be part of the speed fight.

5. In Spades (8-1) is a Parx lifer (39 starts here, 5 wins at the distance) with a high per-start number on Pennsylvania-bred bonuses. Figures 82 then 77; Rangel is 8%.

6. Red Zone Runner (8-1), Freedom's Echo (12-1) and Just a Fair Shake (10-1) all fall short on figures. Red Zone Runner's best dirt-fast figure is a 73, Freedom's Echo is stuck around 74 and will be in the speed duel with a 10-pound apprentice, and Just a Fair Shake (79, 76, 59, 76) has been away since an illness scratch for a barn that is 0-for-5 at the meet.

Bottom line

Dreambuilder at 7-2 is the bet if he holds the price: the top and rising figure, a win at this level, a good trip setup off the speed, and a 25% local combo. Happy Offering at 5-1 is the second choice on per-start money and distance record. Waitlist at 8-1 is the flyer if the speed fight collapses.

# Recap: Woodbine Race 8, Sun Oct 18

**Result:** Magical Trickster won ($6.20, $3.90, $2.10), Marvellous Marvin 2nd, Souper Survivor 3rd. Frost King Stakes, 7 furlongs on the fast synthetic track in 1:23.25 (:22.97, :45.42, 1:09.75). Exacta 4-2 paid $16.60. No scratches.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks

- **Signature Winner (6-1), top pick: 5th of 6.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- **Magical Trickster (4-1), second pick: won.** Win, place and show paid $6.20, $3.90 and $2.10, so the $6 bet returned $12.20 (**+$6.20**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **+$0.20**.

## What happened

The two best horses on figures and form finished 1-2. Magical Trickster, who beat Signature Winner on debut, did it again. The 6-5 favorite Souper Survivor, Signature Winner's stablemate, settled for 3rd. Signature Winner never threatened.

## Grading the handicap

The second pick's case was the right one: best figure among the playable horses (67), a win over the top pick already on the record, and jockey Moran choosing to ride him over Signature Winner. The write-up flagged the top pick's real problem, a top Beyer of 62 against 73 for the favorite, and that gap decided the race.

Note the price: Magical Trickster paid $6.20, meaning he went off near 2-1. That is under the 3.5-to-1 rule, so on the live board he would have been off the list. The tracker grades on the morning line, so this win counts here, but it would not have been a bet at post time.

## Takeaway

When the top pick's best figure trails the contenders by 10 points or more, the "proven at the distance" angle is not enough. A jockey leaving a horse to ride a rival is a tell worth weighting.

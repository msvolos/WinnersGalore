# Recap: Parx Racing Race 8, Tue Oct 20

**Result:** Wild Vine won ($3.40, $2.60, $2.10), Dreambuilder 2nd, Bartlett 3rd. M.P. Ballezzi Appreciation Mile, 1 mile on a fast dirt track in 1:37.31 (:23.84, :46.97, 1:11.49, 1:23.96). Exacta 3-1 paid $16.20. No scratches.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks

- **Margie's Fun Son (6-1), top pick: 4th of 6.** First of the also-rans, so no ticket cashed (**−$6.00**).
- **Duration (5-1), second pick: 5th.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$12.00**.

## What happened

The handicap expected three speed horses (Duration, Freedom's Echo and Bartlett) to burn each other up and hand the race to a closer. The pace never got hot: :46.97 for the half and 1:11.49 for six furlongs is moderate for a one-turn mile stakes. With no collapse, the class of the race won. The 5-2 favorite Wild Vine, the other late-pace horse, won easily. Bartlett, one of the speed horses, kept going for 3rd, and Duration, who wanted the lead, faded to 5th.

## Grading the handicap

The write-up said it plainly: Wild Vine was the most likely winner and no value at 5-2. That was correct. Margie's Fun Son needed a fast pace to have a shot, and he did not get one, though 4th behind the favorite was a fair effort. Bartlett, named as a saver, ran 3rd.

## Takeaway

Third time in four races the projected pace meltdown did not happen. Before backing a closer on pace, check how fast the speed horses have actually gone early in their recent races, not just how many of them there are.

# Recap: Woodbine Race 10, Sat Nov 14

**Result:** War Strategy won ($20.80, $8.90, $4.50), Arbiter 2nd ($7.00, $4.10), Victory Achieved 3rd ($2.80 show). The Grade 2 Kennedy Road Stakes, 6 furlongs on a fast synthetic track in 1:09.77 (:22.37, :44.92, :56.87). Exacta 6-2 paid $75.00, and the 20-cent trifecta 6-2-5 paid $51.98. No scratches.

## Our picks
- **Victory Achieved (2-1), top pick: 3rd.** The show ticket paid $2.80 (**-$3.20**).
- **Arbiter (3-1), second pick: 2nd.** Place and show paid $11.10 (**+$5.10**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **+$1.90**.

## What happened
The write-up called for plenty of speed, and it showed up. Arbiter went to the front on the rail with Nobals pressing two to three wide, and the half went in :44.92. Several rivals drew nearly even with Arbiter at the quarter pole, and he shook clear of them at the three-sixteenths. Victory Achieved was bumped lightly at the start, stalked the leading pair, moved three to four wide on the turn, made a mild bid, was floated out a path or two into the stretch and just missed 2nd. War Strategy was bumped and taken up soon after the start, settled off the pace, cut the corner leaving the turn, tipped out between horses and took over a sixteenth out to draw clear. Arbiter just saved the place. Nobals could not sustain his bid, and My Boy Prince reared at the start and lost his rider.

## Grading the handicap
The two picks ran 2nd and 3rd, both in the trifecta, which is what the figures said. Arbiter came back from five months off and ran big on the front end, and he went off 4.60-1, above his 9-2 bet price, so that bet qualified. Victory Achieved went off 2.10-1, below his 16-5 bet price, and the named risk (sitting in a contested pace from a wide trip) is roughly how it went. The miss was War Strategy, listed in The rest as the flyer. The write-up expected him to use his speed, but he won from off the pace after a rough start, at 9.40-1.

## Takeaway
A small profit on a night when the top pick ran to form at a short price. The flyer note was right about the horse but wrong about how he would run, which is a reminder that early-speed numbers do not guarantee a horse's trip.

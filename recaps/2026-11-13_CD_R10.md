# Recap: Churchill Downs Race 10, Fri Nov 13

**Result:** Kalahari Dreams won ($7.86, $4.26, $2.96), Noises Off 2nd ($6.00, $3.40), Great White 3rd ($3.10 show). A $134k optional claiming allowance (OC 80k/N$Y), taken off the turf and run at 1 mile on a muddy (sealed) dirt track in 1:35.77 (:23.04, :46.14, 1:10.73, 1:23.08). Exacta 13-9 paid $52.66, and the 50-cent trifecta 13-9-5 paid $54.61. Nine horses scratched: Early Adopter, Dr. Agne, First Resort, Cheval de Guerre, War At Sea, Theismann, Restore, No Joke Joe and Tough Little Nut, leaving seven starters.

## What happened
The race came off the turf, and every turf specialist the write-up liked, plus Theismann, came out. Maui Strong set the pace from the rail with Sushi Man pressing two wide, and the half went in :46.14, quick for this group. Noises Off tracked, pressed through the turn, took over inside the three-sixteenths pole and gave in late. Kalahari Dreams was bumped at the start, chased three wide, swung into the six path for the stretch and edged clear under a long drive. Great White saved ground and weakened from the inside to hold 3rd. Maui Strong faded to 5th and Sushi Man folded.

## Grading the handicap
There is not much to grade. The handicap was built for a turf mile and the race became a dirt sprint-to-route on a wet track. Kalahari Dreams was in "The rest" as a sprinter whose trainer is 3% going sprint to route, but on a muddy dirt mile he was dropping back onto his surface, 9 starts at Churchill with five 2nds. Noises Off, ranked fair at 6-1, ran 2nd at 5.53-1. Maui Strong, the horse to beat, went off 3.24-1 and never had his surface.

## Takeaway
When a turf race comes off, the turf handicap is void and the dirt form of the leftovers takes over. Both picks were turf-only horses, so the scratches were no surprise. A no-loss night.

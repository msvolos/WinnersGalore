# Recap: Churchill Downs Race 6, Thu Oct 29

**Result:** Onehotmess won ($12.64, $4.94, $4.00), Struck At Midnight 2nd, Charm of Venice (GB) 3rd. Allowance $127,000 / N1X for fillies and mares, 1 3/8 miles on a firm turf course in 2:14.82 (:25.13, :50.14, 1:14.58, 1:39.29, 2:03.05). Exacta 3-1 paid $58.88 and the trifecta 3-1-6 paid $49.74 (50-cent). No scratches.

## Our picks

- **Marcinkowski (9-2), top pick: 5th.** No return (**−$6.00**).
- **Charm of Venice (GB) (4-1), second pick: 3rd.** Show $2.50 returned $2.50 (**−$3.50**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$9.50**.

## What happened

Marcinkowski brushed with a rival at the start, led along the inside early, then got headed passing the wire the first time when Heart Spin rushed up three wide. Heart Spin led around the second turn and drew clear down the backstretch, with Marcinkowski stalking. Marcinkowski rebid two wide entering the lane but got outkicked and yielded at 8-1. Heart Spin was caught entering the lane and faded to last.

Charm of Venice laid off the pace, steadied behind horses with a mile to go, moved four deep on the final turn and bid for the lead entering the lane. She vied into the final furlong and got outkicked on both sides. She was bet down to 1.17-1, the favorite, so she would not have qualified at post time. Paseo led narrowly into the final furlong and yielded to 4th.

Onehotmess tracked along the inside, waited for room behind the leaders on the far turn, angled out in upper stretch, split horses into the final furlong and wrestled the lead away at the sixteenth pole, holding off Struck At Midnight at 5.32-1. Struck At Midnight swung five wide into the lane and was denied.

## Grading the handicap

The pace read missed. The write-up projected Marcinkowski on a soft, uncontested lead, but Heart Spin, who showed little early speed on paper, went after her before the first turn and the leader never got her own way. The ranking had Onehotmess 3rd and expected her to be bet under 7-2; she went off at 5.32-1 under a different rider (Alvarado, not Irad Ortiz) and won. Struck At Midnight, left off the list at 3-1, ran 2nd at 4.62-1. Charm of Venice ran her race and took 3rd, but she was a short price.

## Takeaway

A "lone speed" read at a marathon trip is fragile: one rival moving early takes away the whole case. Like Twilight Delight the day before, the winner was an improving horse ranked below our picks, and she drifted up to a playable price while our second pick got hammered.

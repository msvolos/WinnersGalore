# Recap: Churchill Downs Race 8, Wed Nov 18

**Result:** Rugged Love won ($17.60, $6.96, $4.32), Pagode 2nd ($3.04, $2.58), King of Ashes 3rd ($5.66 show). A $127,000 allowance for 3-year-olds and up, non-winners of one other than maiden, claiming or starter, 1 1/8 miles on a fast dirt track in 1:48.55 (:24.43, :48.73, 1:12.46, 1:36.24). Exacta 9-4 paid $46.32, and the trifecta 9-4-5 paid $83.89. C McGriff and Uncle Evco scratched.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## What happened
The write-up expected Rugged Love and Discotheque to hook up on the lead and make the pace honest for the stalkers and closers. The fractions say otherwise: a :24.43 quarter and :48.73 half are soft for a 1 1/8 mile allowance, and a horse with Early 114 speed usually gets loose on splits like that. Rugged Love won at about 7.8-1 off his $17.60 win price. Without the chart there is no word on positions or trouble, but Discotheque finished last and the closers named in the write-up (Vitruvian Man 4th, Zapruder 8th) never got there. Pagode, the only horse who had run to par, was the one who came closest, again second.

## Grading the handicap
Half right. Pagode ran his race and hit the board as the top pick, though at 1-for-11 with seven seconds now, the named risk (he keeps finding one better) is exactly what happened. The pace read was wrong: the speed was not compromised and the winner was in The rest as one of the two pace horses, with a 23% Gaffalione/Walsh combo the write-up noted and passed over. Vitruvian Man (horse to beat on figures) and Commentate (big underlay) both missed the board, so those calls held. C McGriff scratched without warning; Uncle Evco's scratch was flagged.

## Takeaway
Another race where a projected pace fight did not happen and the speed held, the same pattern behind the skill's rule that pace is a tiebreaker only. A lone or soft-pace speed horse with a strong J/T deserves a place on the contender list, even at 8-1.

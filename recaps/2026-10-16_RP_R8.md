# Recap: Remington Park Race 8, Fri Oct 16

**Result:** Connors Outlaw won ($47.60, $18.20, $6.40), Easy as Pie 2nd, Cajun Stoops 3rd. Oklahoma Classics Sprint, 6 furlongs on a fast dirt track in 1:10.69 (:22.05, :45.41, :57.89). Exacta 3-1 paid $376.20. No scratches.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks

- **Easy as Pie (9-2), top pick: 2nd.** Place and show paid $6.80 and $6.20, so the $6 bet returned $13.00 (**+$7.00**).
- **Missin Hollywood (8-1), second pick: 11th of 11.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **+$1.00**.

## What happened

The handicap expected a speed fight: Guapo, Legacy Account, Connors Outlaw and Lil Elvis all wanted the lead, which looked like a setup for horses sitting off the pace. The fractions were quick but not crazy for a 6-furlong stakes, and one of those speed horses, Connors Outlaw, held on to win at about 23-1. The closer we liked as the second pick, Missin Hollywood, finished last.

## Grading the handicap

The top pick was right about the horse and wrong about the race. Easy as Pie ran to his rising form and beat everyone but the winner, which is exactly what a place and show ticket is for. The pace read missed: the race did not fall apart, and the winner came from the group we expected to tire. Connors Outlaw's per-start earnings ($2,486 this year) were the weakest of the contenders, so the earnings screen never had him on the radar.

## Takeaway

A crowded speed map at 6 furlongs does not guarantee a collapse. At short sprint distances, speed often holds, so a closer should not get a big upgrade on pace projection alone.

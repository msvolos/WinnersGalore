# Recap: Keeneland Race 9, Sat Oct 17

**Result:** Movie Night won ($9.84, $5.56, $4.48), Mythical 2nd, Army's Marauder 3rd. Grade 2 Raven Run, 7 furlongs on a fast dirt track in 1:23.28 (:22.75, :45.84, 1:10.34). Exacta 11-2 paid $93.58 and the trifecta $1,621.80. Yo Fancy scratched.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks

- **Conceit (4-1), top pick: 4th.** First of the also-rans, so no ticket cashed (**−$6.00**).
- **Percy's Bar (6-1), second pick: 9th.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$12.00**.

## What happened

The handicap called Mashallah and Movie Night the two fastest early horses and expected them to soften each other up for a stalker like Conceit. Instead, the pace was moderate for a Keeneland 7-furlong stakes (:45.84 for the half), and the race went to the speed. Movie Night won at about 4-1, and Mythical, who was also expected to press, ran 2nd at a big price. The 8-5 favorite Mashallah faded to 5th. Percy's Bar, the horse with the best late-pace number in the field, never got involved.

## Grading the handicap

Conceit ran close to her figures and missed the board by one spot, so the read on her was fair. The miss was the pace. A two-horse speed duel needs both horses to go hard, and only one of them kept going. Percy's Bar was picked largely for a pace setup that never came.

## Takeaway

For the second race in a row, a speed horse we flagged held on. When the projected pace depends on two specific horses dueling, treat that as a guess, and do not lean on late-pace numbers unless the early fractions are likely to be fast.

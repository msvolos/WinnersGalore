# Recap: Parx Racing Race 8, Tue Nov 10

**Result:** Lady Annabelle won ($3.80, $2.60, $2.10), Nature's Candy 2nd ($3.20, $2.40), Mary Q 3rd ($2.10 show). The $75k Disco Chick Stakes for fillies and mares, 7 furlongs on a fast dirt track in 1:25.36 (:23.19, :46.55, 1:12.34). Exacta 1-5 paid $13.60, and the 50-cent trifecta 1-5-2 paid $5.25. Angie's Reward scratched.

## Our picks
- **Nature's Candy (3-1), top pick: 2nd.** Returned $5.60 ($3.20 place, $2.40 show) (**-$0.40**). She went off at 3.10-1, below the 9-2 the write-up asked for, though above the 2-1 floor.
- **Mary Q (4-1), second pick: 3rd.** Returned $2.10 on the show ticket (**-$3.90**). She went off at 2.40-1, well under the 6-1 she needed.
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$4.30**.

## What happened
The write-up expected three early-speed types up front, and the chart backs that up. Aoife's Magic set the pace on the inside, with Nature's Candy close up two wide and Lady Annabelle tracking on the rail. Nature's Candy bid on the turn and took over as Aoife's Magic faded after a duel. Lady Annabelle angled out on the turn, rallied three wide into the stretch, charged to the front at the furlong marker and pulled away, while Nature's Candy dug in gamely inside for a clear 2nd. Mary Q stumbled at the start and bumped Carousel Queen, was outrun early, then rallied on the rail for 3rd. Presenceisapresent chased three wide and tired. The half in :46.55 was honest but not a meltdown, so the closer only got up for the show.

## Grading the handicap
The ranking was right: the horse to beat won, and our two picks ran 2nd and 3rd, which is the exact order the fair-odds table had them. The call that Lady Annabelle was too short to bet held up too, since she paid $3.80. Nature's Candy ran her race and the write-up's excuse for the last-out 72 looks fair; she just met the same mare who beat her a month ago. The weights moving her way did not close the gap.

## Takeaway
In a short field with a dominant favorite, the method can read the race correctly and still lose money on WPS. Both picks were bet down below the write-up's own target prices (9-2 and 6-1). Nature's Candy still cleared the 2-1 floor, so the post-time odds rule would have bet her and lost 40 cents; the stricter per-horse targets would have passed the race.

# Recap: Churchill Downs Race 9, Wed Nov 4

**Result:** Being Myself won ($13.08, $6.34, $3.98), Shilling 2nd, Athaliah 3rd. Optional claiming $80,000 / allowance N2X for fillies and mares, 1 1/16 miles on a sloppy (sealed) dirt track in 1:46.09 (:24.60, :49.33, 1:13.83, 1:39.35). Exacta 5-10 paid $56.00 and the 50-cent trifecta 5-10-2 paid $126.54. No scratches.

## Our picks

- **Ile de La Cite (5-1), top pick: 6th.** Returned $0 (**-$6.00**). She went off at 13.33-1.
- **Resist (8-1), second pick: 5th.** Returned $0 (**-$6.00**). She went off at 6.08-1.
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$12.00**.

## What happened

The rain changed the race. The track came up sloppy and sealed, and the pace was moderate, not the speed fight the write-up projected: :24.60 and :49.33 with 20-1 Rando on the lead in the two path and Tapit Quick pressing. Being Myself tracked from midpack along the inside, swung out to the five path at the five-sixteenths pole, dueled with Shilling through the final furlong and got up late. Shilling stalked in the four path and led in midstretch before getting edged. The flyer Athaliah ran 3rd after she lacked room on the rail at the furlong grounds and had to be altered out.

Ile de La Cite, the horse expected to be part of the early speed, was 10th of 10 at the start and raced at the rear in the four to six path into the backstretch, then gained only late. Resist brushed with a rival leaving the gate, tracked along the inside, chased turning for home and stayed on mildly. Clairita, the 1.61-1 favorite, brushed at the start, raced wide and ran 9th.

## Grading the handicap

The write-up had the winner ranked 3rd and said she qualified only if she stayed 7-2 or longer. She went off at 5.54-1, so the price call was right, but she was ranked behind both picks because of her falling figures and a 0-for-17 jockey/trainer stat. The pace projection missed: the race had no speed duel, and our speed horse never got into it. Athaliah as the flyer was a good call. The write-up's case for Ile de La Cite was all fast-track form, and she had never raced on a wet track.

## Takeaway

A sloppy track turned a speed-heavy projection into a stalker's race, and a top pick with no wet form was a risk the write-up never named. When the surface comes up off, a horse proven over it (Being Myself was 2nd in the slop here in May) deserves a second look before post.

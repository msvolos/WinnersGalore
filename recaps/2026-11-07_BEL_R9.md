# Recap: Belmont Park Race 9, Sat Nov 7

**Result:** Tap to Open won ($12.72, $5.52, $3.70), Answered Prayers 2nd, Tahlequah 3rd. The Grade 2 Mother Goose Stakes, 1 1/16 miles on a fast dirt track in 1:41.72 (:23.19, :45.94, 1:10.62, 1:35.43). The $1 exacta 3-2 paid $31.05 and the 50-cent trifecta 3-2-9 paid $181.16. Scratched: Cognition, Laken, Ivy Girl.

## Our picks

- **Bodacious Bay (5-2), top pick: 4th.** Returned $0 (**-$6.00**). She went off at 0.84-1, well under the 13-5 the write-up asked for and under the 2-1 floor, so the bet would not have qualified at post.
- **Tap to Open (5-1), second pick: 1st.** Returned $21.94 ($12.72 win, $5.52 place, $3.70 show) (**+$15.94**). She went off at 5.36-1.
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **+$9.94**.

## What happened

The write-up expected a real pace with several speed horses, and that is what came. Iron Orchard went to the front, and Bodacious Bay prompted her from the outside, took the lead near the nine-sixteenths pole and vied with Iron Orchard in the two path around the turn. Those two went :45.94 and 1:10.62, an honest clip for a two-turn route. Bodacious Bay was given her cue inside the five-sixteenths, spun into the stretch three wide, lost the lead at the three-sixteenths pole and tired to 4th. Iron Orchard faded to last.

Tap to Open did not use her speed. Franco rated her off the pace, saved ground on the turn, moved to the two path late on that bend, rallied to the lead in upper stretch and drew clear under strong urging. Answered Prayers settled, swung six wide into the lane and closed for the place. Tahlequah tracked, made a bid near the three-sixteenths and weakened but held 3rd.

## Grading the handicap

The ranking held up well. Both picks were in the top two, Answered Prayers (ranked 5th) ran 2nd, and the risk named for the top pick, a step back after a career-best G1 win, is what happened once she got into a duel. The read on Tap to Open as a pace-fighter was wrong; she sat back and the pace setup favored her. The sloppy-track angle did not matter, since the track came up fast. The board made Bodacious Bay a heavy favorite, and the write-up's own rule said no bet at that price. It also asked about 7-1 on Tap to Open, so at 5.36-1 the write-up would have passed the race entirely.

## Takeaway

When the top pick gets bet to odds-on, the price rule saves the $6, and the second pick becomes the live ticket. The 7-1 ask on Tap to Open was too strict here; the simpler post-time odds rule (2-1 floor) bets her and cashes.

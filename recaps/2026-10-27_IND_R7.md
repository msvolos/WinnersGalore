# Recap: Horseshoe Indianapolis Race 7, Tue Oct 27

**Result:** Collective Beauty won ($5.40, $3.20, $2.20), Surprenant Cocca 2nd, Cinquante Cinq (Ire) 3rd. Allowance N1X for fillies and mares, 1 mile on a firm turf course in 1:35.26 (:23.35, :46.80, 1:10.84, 1:22.88). Exacta 3-10 paid $76.20 and the trifecta 3-10-7 paid $62.90 (50-cent). Scratched: Ring Rights, Screamin Nina, Dreaming of Joy, Max, Temple Temptress, Donna Strada.

## Our picks

- **Never Really Know (Ire) (8-1), top pick: 7th.** No return (**−$6.00**).
- **Cinquante Cinq (Ire) (9-2), second pick: 3rd.** Show $2.40 returned $2.40 (**−$3.60**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$9.60**.

## What happened

Stormy Paradise broke sharply and dueled outside Collective Beauty through :46.80 and 1:10.84. Collective Beauty dueled inside, bid near the quarter pole and drew off under a steady drive at about 1.70-1. Surprenant Cocca stalked between rivals and bid in early stretch but was no match. Stormy Paradise gave way in early stretch and finished 9th.

Never Really Know stalked the pace in the three path and gave way near the quarter pole, beaten for 7th at 8.60-1. Cinquante Cinq broke 9th of 10, saved ground, angled to the four path in early stretch and made a mild late bid for 3rd. She was bet down to 1.80-1, so she would not have qualified at post time. Sudden Rise, the flyer, was squeezed back at the start and only improved late for 5th.

## Grading the handicap

The scratch calls were all right: Dreaming of Joy, Max, Ring Rights and Screamin Nina all came out. The write-up named Collective Beauty the class of the field and "the speed of the speed," and that was correct. It expected her to get hung out in a speed fight, but she only had Stormy Paradise to deal with, the fractions were honest rather than fast, and she was never threatened late. Never Really Know had a fair stalking trip and simply was not good enough. The class step from claiming company was the named risk, and it happened. The write-up also flagged that Cinquante Cinq could go under 7-2, and she went off at 1.80-1.

## Takeaway

The short-priced class horse with the top recent figure (75, three points from par) won when the pace never collapsed. As in earlier recaps, the projected speed duel did not set up the closers, and a top pick stepping up in class off claiming figures came up short.

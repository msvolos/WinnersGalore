# Recap: Keeneland Race 7, Thu Oct 22

**Result:** Atropa won ($5.86, $4.42, $3.30), Maria Louise 2nd, Leinani 3rd. Fillies and mares Allowance N1X ($120,000), 1 1/16 miles on a fast dirt track in 1:44.07 (:24.26, :48.53, 1:12.92, 1:37.65). Exacta 6-2 paid $17.74 and the trifecta $53.27 (50-cent). No scratches.

## Our picks

- **Passionfruit (6-1), top pick: 8th.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- **Athenix (10-1), second pick: 9th.** Returned $0 (**−$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$12.00**.

## What happened

American Pearl led narrowly with Atropa pressing from the outside, and the two vied around the far turn through an honest pace (:48.53 for the half, 1:12.92 for six furlongs). Atropa drew clear in upper stretch and held sway at about 2-1. Maria Louise stalked along the inside, swung four wide in the lane and ran on for 2nd. Leinani rallied from midpack on the rail for 3rd at almost 5-1.

Passionfruit pursued from midpack in the five to four path and tired. The board did not like her either: she drifted from 6-1 to about 13-1. Athenix laid off the pace three and four wide, fanned out to the six path entering the lane and never factored. Ivory and Ebony trailed, saved ground and moved up late for 5th.

## Grading the handicap

This was a miss. The write-up projected a duel between Maria Louise and American Pearl. The pressure on American Pearl came from the favorite, and Maria Louise sat behind that fight and benefited. The top pick's rising figures did not hold up against the deeper horses, and her weak connections (Sheehy 0 for 27 at the meet) looked like the real story. Athenix's flagged risks, the class jump and the first try at 1 1/16, both showed up. Atropa was off the list on the odds rule and went off below 2-1, so no qualifying bet would have caught her. Maria Louise, ranked fourth at 4-1, ran 2nd at 5-1, and Leinani, called too slow, ran 3rd.

## Takeaway

Freddie B won yesterday on figures already at par. Passionfruit's best was an 80 against a par of 85, and an improving horse still below par is a weaker play than one already there. A weak barn plus a drifting price is worth respecting.

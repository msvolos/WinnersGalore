# Recap: Finger Lakes Race 7, Mon Nov 16

**Result:** Hey Pal won ($5.22, $3.22, $2.56), Isola d'Oro 2nd ($3.90, $2.84), Start the Day 3rd ($2.92 show). A $26,900 allowance for non-winners of three, 1 mile 70 yards on a good (sealed) dirt track in 1:44.14 (:24.69, :48.94, 1:13.15, 1:39.78). Exacta 2-4 paid $17.12, and the 50-cent trifecta 2-4-6 paid $14.63. No scratches.

## Our picks
- **Hey Pal (3-1), top pick: won.** Win, place and show paid $11.00 (**+$5.00**).
- **Isola d'Oro (4-1), second pick: 2nd.** Place and show paid $6.74 (**+$0.74**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **+$5.74**.

## What happened
The pace went as the write-up drew it. Fess was sent to the lead along the three path and set moderate fractions (:24.69, :48.94), with Hey Pal tracking him in second, four wide, and Start the Day just behind them five wide. Fess gave way on the far turn. Hey Pal took over a furlong out and drove clear, which answered the write-up's worry about him getting caught late. Isola d'Oro stayed close along the two path, saved ground into the lane, challenged with a furlong to go and finished well for 2nd. Start the Day finished well for 3rd. Serra, unhurried along the three path, failed to fire and was 4th. Inonit hopped at the start and was never a factor.

## Grading the handicap
A clean hit. The two picks ran 1-2, and the top four in the fair-odds table filled the first four spots, with Hey Pal and Isola d'Oro on top, then Start the Day and Serra. The close-call tiebreak on win chance picked the right horse. The Serra call held: the 5-2 "underlay" with the best figure seven below par needed the speed to come back, it didn't, and he did nothing. One miss: the write-up expected Serra to be the favorite, but the crowd made Hey Pal the choice. Neither bet qualified at post time. Hey Pal went off 1.61-1, below the 2-1 floor and the 9-2 bet price, and Isola d'Oro went off 3.70-1 against an 11-2 bet price.

## Takeaway
The figure horse at par beat the lightly raced closer on the morning-line favorite's spot, and the method ranked the field in order. The cost is familiar from Woodbine: when the method is right, the crowd usually agrees, and the price rules keep us off.

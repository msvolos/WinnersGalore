# Recap: Parx Racing Race 8, Mon Oct 19

**Result:** Houghton Shuffle won ($7.60, $3.60, $2.40), Subrogate 2nd, Winning Time 3rd. Optional claiming allowance ($100,000), 6 furlongs on a fast dirt track in 1:10.01 (:22.52, :45.81, :57.69). Exacta 4-2 paid $54.20. No scratches.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks

- **Maximus Meridius (7-2), top pick: 4th of 6.** First of the also-rans, so no ticket cashed (**−$6.00**).
- **Subrogate (5-1), second pick: 2nd.** Place and show paid $7.40 and $4.40, so the $6 bet returned $11.80 (**+$5.80**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$0.20**.

## What happened

This one went the way the handicap's own warning said it might. The write-up named the risk directly: Spikezone and Winning Time both wanted the lead, and a three-way duel would set it up for the closer Houghton Shuffle. The 5-2 favorite came home first, Winning Time hung on for 3rd, and Maximus Meridius, part of that early group, faded to 4th.

## Grading the handicap

The pace read was right, but the top pick was the horse that pace hurt. Subrogate's class case held up: he shook off a dull 72 in his only start this year and ran 2nd at 5-1, which nearly paid for both bets. Houghton Shuffle was off the list at 5-2, so the race's likeliest winner was never a play.

## Takeaway

When the write-up's biggest named risk is "this pace sets up the favorite," the top pick is fragile. In that spot the second choice deserves equal weight, and the Top 2 strategy nearly broke even here because of it.

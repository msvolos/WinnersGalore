# Recap: Woodbine Race 10, Sun Nov 15

**Result:** Bond Market won ($5.20, $3.20, $2.10), My Covenant 2nd ($3.40, $2.30), Imyourmedicineman 3rd ($2.30 show). The $150k Lake Ontario Stakes for Ontario-bred 3-year-olds, 1 1/16 miles on a fast synthetic track in 1:44.60 (:24.19, :48.68, 1:12.84, 1:37.86). Exacta 2-4 paid $8.50 for $1, and the 20-cent trifecta 2-4-6 paid $3.42. No scratches.

## Our picks
- **My Covenant (5-2), top pick: 2nd.** Place and show paid $5.70 (**-$0.30**).
- **Imyourmedicineman (7-2), second pick: 3rd.** The show ticket paid $2.30 (**-$3.70**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$4.00**.

## What happened
The write-up made My Covenant the lone speed, and he got exactly that trip. He was loose on the lead just off the rail, set an honest pace (:24.19, :48.68) unchallenged down the backstretch and around the far turn, and still had the lead turning for home. Bond Market was unhurried at the back on the fence early, moved up on the rail down the backside, sat waiting behind the leader at the five-sixteenths, then swung four wide at the top of the stretch, caught My Covenant outside in the final furlong and pulled away late. My Covenant held on alone for a gutsy 2nd. Imyourmedicineman was shuffled back at the half, made a sharp four-wide move at the quarter pole, swung out six wide into the stretch and ran on for a clear 3rd. Decadent Danish tracked three wide and flattened out for 4th. A rider's claim of foul against Imyourmedicineman around the first turn was disallowed.

## Grading the handicap
The fair-odds table had the right three on top: My Covenant 32%, Bond Market 24%, Imyourmedicineman 18%, and those three filled the trifecta. The miss was the top spot, where the first two swapped. Bond Market was called the horse to beat on class, and the class held up despite the 8% rider and the cutback. The named risk on My Covenant, his first try past 7 furlongs, showed up late: he got his easy lead and still could not hold off the closer. Neither bet qualified at post time. My Covenant went off 2.05-1 against a 3-1 bet price, and Imyourmedicineman 2.50-1 against 6-1. Bond Market went off 1.60-1, under the 2-1 floor.

## Takeaway
A chalky race where the method ranked the field correctly and the price rules kept us off the winner. Like Lady Annabelle at Prairie Meadows, the horse to beat under the floor won; the floor costs us these, by design.

# Recap: Belmont Park Race 7, Thu Nov 12

**Result:** Stand the Rain won ($4.56, $2.82, $2.18), Abigail 2nd ($4.04, $3.64), Alibi 3rd ($2.52 show). An optional claiming allowance for fillies and mares (OC 55k/N1X), taken off the turf and run at 1 3/16 miles on a fast all-weather track in 1:57.33 (:24.52, :50.07, 1:15.37, 1:39.60). Exacta 4-5 paid $9.37, and the trifecta 4-5-2 paid $10.47. Consensus View and Toes scratched, leaving five starters.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks
- **Bobby M's Girl (12-1), top pick: 4th of 5.** Nothing back (**-$6.00**).
- **Stand the Rain (5-2), second pick: won.** $4.56 + $2.82 + $2.18 = $9.56 back (**+$3.56**). She went off at about 1.3-1, well under the 4-1 the write-up asked for and under the 2-1 floor, so the bet would not have qualified at post time.
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$2.44**.

## What happened
The race came off the turf, moved to the all-weather track at a shorter distance, and lost two of its seven runners. Toes, the only real speed, and Consensus View, the second-best figure horse, both scratched. That left a five-horse field with no clear pace. The fractions were slow (:50.07 for the half, 1:15.37 for six furlongs), which is what the write-up feared for a deep closer like Bobby M's Girl. Without the chart there is no trip detail, but the order says the slow pace helped the horses who were closer to it.

## Grading the handicap
The write-up warned at the top: "If rain takes it off the turf, expect scratches and ignore this read." That is what happened. Even so, its most likely winner, Stand the Rain, won, and the favorite logic held once Consensus View was gone. Bobby M's Girl's case needed pace to close into, and the lone speed scratched. Abigail (8-1, in The rest) ran 2nd, and Alibi, called the big underlay, ran 3rd in a five-horse field, which says little.

## Takeaway
When a turf race comes off and the field shrinks, the morning read is stale. A top pick that depends on pace should come with a check on whether the speed actually runs.

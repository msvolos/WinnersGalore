# Recap: Parx Racing Race 8, Tue Nov 3

**Result:** Preacha Meyers won ($5.60, $3.20, $2.10), Red Zone Runner 2nd, Waitlist 3rd. Optional claiming $50,000 / allowance, 1 mile 70 yards on a fast dirt track. Exacta 6-4 paid $15.30 per $1 and the trifecta 6-4-2 paid $13.25 per 50 cents. Just a Fair Shake, Big Blue Line, Dreambuilder and In Spades scratched, leaving five starters.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was not shown on the race's PP page when this recap was written (only the mutuel prices were up), so it uses the official result only. The final time, fractions and also-ran order were not posted yet either.*

## Our picks

- **Dreambuilder (7-2), top pick: scratched.** The $6 bet is refunded (**$0.00**).
- **Happy Offering (5-1), second pick: 5th of five** (last; the final also-ran order had Freedom's Echo 4th). The $6 bet returned nothing (**−$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **−$6.00**.

## What happened

Four scratches turned a nine-horse race into a five-horse race and took out our top pick, Big Blue Line and In Spades. That also thinned the speed. The write-up expected a contested half with Happy Offering, Freedom's Echo, Big Blue Line, Preacha Meyers and Red Zone Runner all wanting to be close. Without the chart there is no record of how the pace actually played out, so this recap does not guess at positions. What is clear: the favorite won, the speedy Red Zone Runner held 2nd, and the closer Waitlist got up for 3rd. Preacha Meyers paid $5.60, so he went off around 1.8-1, shorter than his 3-1 morning line.

## Grading the handicap

The write-up named Preacha Meyers the horse to beat and left him off the list only on price, and he won. Waitlist, the flyer on best late pace, hit the board. The miss was Red Zone Runner, ranked near the bottom on figures (best dirt-fast Beyer 73), who ran 2nd. The write-up also said "no probable scratches" and four horses came out, including Dreambuilder. Happy Offering's named risk was finding one too many late after three straight 2nd- or 3rd-place finishes as the favorite. This time he did not even hit the board in a small field.

## Takeaway

When a field shrinks to five, the place and show pools get small and the favorite's edge grows. A late-scratched race like this one is a good spot to pass the remaining pick unless it still clears the 3.5-1 line on the board.

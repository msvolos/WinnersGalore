## What happened

Freddie B delivered at about 4-1. The pace was honest but not fast (:48.66 for the half, 1:13.54 for six furlongs), and he still had the class to win. C McGriff, a 20-1 shot, ran 2nd, and the flyer Rugged Love held 3rd. Pagode, off the list at 5-2, ran 4th, again finding a way to miss. Fuzzbuster scratched, just as the write-up predicted from his cross-entry.

## Grading the handicap

The core read was right. Freddie B had run 91, 90 and 88 at this exact class and matched the par, and the earnings and connections screens backed him up. The write-up called the scratch correctly and had Rugged Love as the flyer. Bourbon Flight's flagged risks (the class jump and post 11 in a two-turn route) showed up. The one real miss was C McGriff, dismissed with the slower group, who ran 2nd at a big price.

## Takeaway

This is the method's best case: a top pick whose figures already sit at par for the class, at a fair price, with live connections. Picks built on a pace setup have struggled. Picks built on figures that are already good enough have been the ones to cash.

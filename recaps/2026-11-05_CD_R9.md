# Recap: Churchill Downs Race 9, Thu Nov 5

**Result:** Zimmer won ($3.74, $2.88, $2.34), Don's Winner 2nd, Sure to Appeal 3rd. Allowance N1X ($127k), 1 mile on a fast dirt track in 1:35.84 (:22.36, :44.54, 1:09.12, 1:22.32). Exacta 1-11 paid $18.14 and the 50-cent trifecta 1-11-9 paid $53.26. Scratched: Charlie Foxtrot, Spirit of Royal, Pimlott, Commentate and Blue Forty Two.

## Our picks

- **Penalty Box (9-2), top pick: 10th.** Returned $0 (**-$6.00**). He went off at 4.58-1.
- **Don's Winner (6-1), second pick: 2nd.** Returned $9.64 ($5.52 place, $4.12 show) (**+$3.64**). He went off at 6.12-1.
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$2.36**.

## What happened

The pace was quick for a mile, :22.36 and :44.54, and Zimmer owned it. He was brushed and last away at the start, recovered to set the pace into the backstretch, met a challenge from Bridgesong at the three furlong pole, drew away in upper stretch and held off a late run. Don's Winner raced off the pace in the three to four path between horses, swung five wide into the lane and was closing in late but ran out of ground. Sure to Appeal lacked early speed along the inside, split horses five wide into the lane and ran on for 3rd.

Penalty Box never got into the speed fight the write-up worried about. He tracked from the four path and weakened between horses in upper stretch to finish 10th. The two scratches that took away pace (Charlie Foxtrot) and our flyer (Pimlott) left Zimmer with only Bridgesong to deal with early.

## Grading the handicap

The write-up named Zimmer the horse to beat and he was, at 0.87-1, off the list on the odds rule. The read on Don's Winner was right: a rising router who would sit off the speed and close, and he did, paying a solid place and show price. Penalty Box was the wrong top pick. His 91 was the best figure among the playable horses, but he ran nowhere near it. The Spirit of Royal scratch call held. Sure to Appeal, dismissed as 19 under par, hit the board for the Brian Lynch barn at 21-1.

## Takeaway

When a short-priced class horse is off the list, the best value often comes from a closer who can pick up the pieces underneath him. That is the second time in recent races (Willy D's at Keeneland, now Don's Winner) the second pick saved the race for the Top 2 strategy.

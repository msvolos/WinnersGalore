# Recap: Fair Grounds Race 8, Thu Nov 19

**Result:** Hes Steelin Mylady won ($4.40, $3.00, $2.40), Lucky Spot 2nd ($11.80, $5.40), Touch Money 3rd ($2.80 show). The Joseph R. Peluso Memorial, a $100,000 stakes for Louisiana-bred 2-year-olds, 6 furlongs on a fast dirt track in 1:10.32 (:21.76, :44.98, :57.05). Exacta 2-10 paid $60.70, and the trifecta 2-10-9 paid $112.20. Levee Native scratched.

*DRF's free chart with trip notes was no longer available when this recap was written, so it uses the official result only.*

## Our picks
- **Yo' Lion (8-1), top pick: 9th (last).** Out of the money (**-$6.00**).
- **Star of Bethlehem (7-2), second pick: 4th.** Out of the money (**-$6.00**).
- Top 2 Picks net for the race: **-$12.00**.

## What happened
The write-up flagged four horses with early speed (Essential George, Lucky Spot, Yo' Lion and Star of Bethlehem), and the fractions confirm a real fight: :21.76 and :44.98 are fast for 2-year-old state-breds. Hes Steelin Mylady, the seasoned horse with three thirds in tougher company, won at about 1.2-1 off his $4.40 win price, so the crowd hammered him from his 9-2 morning line. Lucky Spot (12-1) still held 2nd, and Touch Money, the flyer, ran 3rd. Without the chart there is no word on positions or trouble, but Yo' Lion finished last, and Star of Bethlehem and Essential George ran 4th and 5th.

## Grading the handicap
Mostly wrong on the picks, right on the field. The winner was on the contender list at 20% and rated a hair better on value than Star of Bethlehem (1.10 vs 1.08); the close-call tiebreak on win chance sent the second-pick spot the wrong way. The named risk on Yo' Lion (the 85 was a one-race wonder and he would not get an easy lead) is exactly what happened: two starts since the 85, two bad races. Essential George, the underlay, missed the board, so that call held. Touch Money hitting the board as the flyer was a good read. Lucky Spot (53 maiden claimer win) was dismissed and ran 2nd.

## Takeaway
A top pick resting on one standout figure, with a bad line thrown out, is a fragile case; the horse with three solid efforts in tougher company was the real play. Hes Steelin Mylady also took money hard (9-2 to 6-5), the same signal the skill notes: picks that take money hit the board far more often than drifters.

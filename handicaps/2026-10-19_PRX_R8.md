## Playable horses, ranked

**Maximus Meridius (7-2)** – the play, right on the line. Per-start of $19,580 this year (4 starts) and $31,056 last year (8 starts, 4 wins, Parx Sprint 3rd at 97). Trend is up: Beyers 88 (won the Banjo Picker over Houghton Shuffle), 85 (game 3rd in the State Rep), then 92 when 4th in the $150K Stable Recovery last out, the best figure he has run this year. I throw out the February 61 (checked hard to avoid a foe). He is 7-for-15 at Parx and 5-for-10 at the distance, so nothing to project. Connections are the best in the field: Sanchez wins 28% at the meet, Reid 20%, and the pair are 32% together at Parx with a $1.77 ROI. Drops from two stakes to an allowance. The risk is pace: Spikezone and Winning Time both want the lead too, and a three-way duel sets it up for Houghton Shuffle. He is also the horse most likely to get bet below 7-2.

**Subrogate (5-1)** – the class shipper. Only one start this year, so lean on 2025: $36,375 per start (4 starts, a win in the De Francis at 93, 2nd in the Mr. Prospector). Career top 106 and multiple graded-stakes tries. The trend is the problem: 89, 82, 82, then a dull 72 at Colonial in August, his only start this year, and he has been off eight weeks since with slow farm works. Lopez is 21% here and the Duarte/Lopez combo is 32% with a $1.80 ROI, which is real. Stalking style (early 95) fits a race with this much speed. First start at Parx.

**Winning Time (9-2)** – the in-form speed. $16,807 per start this year, $16,884 in 2024 (missed all of 2025). Just won an N2R allowance by 7 1/2 in the slop (88), and ran 91 winning at 7 furlongs in June. 14 starts, all at Parx. The minuses stack up though: he is moving up from N2R allowance company to this level, his one try against these (the Banjo Picker) ended in a long duel and a 4th, he needs the lead with Spikezone faster early, and the connections are cold (Gonzalez 13%, DeMasi 14%, J/T 10%, and DeMasi off a win is 5% with a $0.76 ROI).

**Dropline (6-1)** – pass. $15,602 per start in 2025, but his only 2026 start ended with a fall in February, he has scratched twice since (vet, then illness on Oct 8), and his latest works are slow (:53 4/5, last of 55). Mosco's +180 days angle (27%, $4.96 ROI) is the only reason to look. Too many question marks.

## Bottom line

Maximus Meridius at 7-2 is the win play: rising figures, a Parx specialist, the best jockey/trainer combo in the race, and a drop in class. If he goes off below 7-2, he falls off the list and the play moves to Subrogate at 5-1 or better (class and a hot combo, but you need to forgive the 72). Winning Time is a small saver only if he gets loose early, which is unlikely with Spikezone inside him.

Neither pick is an overlay on the morning line. Pagode is the top pick (value ratio 1.17, best on the board, and the highest win chance) and becomes a bet at 4-1 or better. C McGriff is the second choice (1.08, a close call that went to Pagode on win chance) and becomes a bet at 10-1 or better.

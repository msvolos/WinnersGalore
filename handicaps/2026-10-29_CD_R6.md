## Playable horses, ranked

**1. Marcinkowski (9-2).** The best figure in the field. She ran an 83 winning a 1 1/2-mile maiden race at Kentucky Downs on the front end ("pace, repelled foe, best"), then a 79 in the Keeneland N1X with Paseo and Charm of Venice, stalking and yielding late at 1 3/16 on good ground. Saez is 17% (2-for-12 at the meet); Victoria Oliver is a weak 12% this year and 9% on turf, which is the knock, though the pair are 18% together. Risk: she carries 123 to the 3-year-olds' 118, she is 0-for-2 on CD turf (61 top), and if someone goes with her early the stamina edge could disappear. Price and pace are the case.

Irish maiden winner going about 1 3/8 ("led final 110 yards, won going away"), then 66 in the KD Oaks (G3) off a stumble at the start, then 79 in the Keeneland N1X after being bumped at the start and swinging seven wide into the lane ("willing"). Risk: she needs pace to run at, and this race may not have any.

**3. Onehotmess (7-2).** Right on the line and likely to get bet below it. The question is the jump from maidens to a $127K N1X in her first start for these connections at CD, and she gives five pounds to the 3-year-olds.

**4. Heart Spin (15-1).** A 6-year-old mare who is 1-for-23, but CD turf is her course (8 1 1 1, $113,197) and she has hit the board twice in three tries at this distance (79). Ran a 72 rallying from six wide for 2nd in a $50K claimer here Sep 17, and she ran an 80 in this N1X condition last November. Navarrete is 12%; the pair are 15% at CD with a $4.32 ROI. Flyer only.

**5. Ring Rights (30-1).** Best figure 68, recent 62 and 59, trained by a 13% barn that is 0-for-3 on the dirt-to-turf angle. Toss.

## Bottom line

Marcinkowski at 9-2 is the play: the top figure in the race, the only proven marathoner, and the one horse who should control the pace. Charm of Venice at 4-1 is the second choice and the closer to use with her, since she has the excuse last time and the better barn. Onehotmess is a fair third but will probably go off below 7-2. Heart Spin at 15-1 is a small flyer on her CD turf record. If Marcinkowski drops under 7-2 on the board, pass her and move to Charm of Venice.

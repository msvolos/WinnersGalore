## Bottom line

San Siro at 6-1 is the play: two straight wins at par, proven at 1 1/8, and the best late kick in a race loaded with speed. Willy D's at 8-1 is the second choice and the one to save with if the speed holds. Tumbarumba at 12-1 is a small flyer on the hot Lynch first-time angle. Stars and Stripes (3-1) is the horse to beat. Check the board: Hit Show at 4-1 could get bet under 7-2 by post time.

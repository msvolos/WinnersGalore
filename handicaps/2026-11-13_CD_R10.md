## The contenders

With no par shown, the reference point is the field itself. The best recent turf figures are Theismann's 94 and 90 (May and June, in OC 175k company), War At Sea's 93 in August, and the 86 to 88 group of Noises Off, Maui Strong and Tough Little Nut.

**Maui Strong (7-2)** is the most likely winner. He ran 87 last out, game in 2nd at this level over this course and distance, after an 86 winning an N1X here in June, with a wide-trip 82 in between at Saratoga. He is 6-2-1-1 on the Churchill turf and 7-2-1-1 at the distance. The problem is the combo: Corrales (13%) and Romans (18% at the meet) are 1-for-23 together at Churchill (4%) and 5% overall, under the 10% floor, so he cannot be the top pick. About 20%; fair 4-1, and 7-2 gives 0.90. Underlay, and the horse to beat.

**Theismann (5-1)** has the best figures in the race, 94 and 90 in OC 175k races here in May and June, and he has early speed (113) nobody else matches. He was claimed by Mike Maker out of a 72 when he popped the gate and faltered on Sep 7, and Maker wins 21% first off the claim. The knocks: Maker is 0-for-19 at this meet, he faded in both of those big-figure races, and he has not run in nine weeks (scratched at Indiana in October). Gaffalione (14%) and Maker are 13% together at Churchill. About 18%; fair 4.6-1, and 5-1 gives 1.08. Fair. Bet him at 6-1 or better.

**Noises Off (6-1)** is the steady one: 88 in an OC 80k N2X at Kentucky Downs (3rd, 4-5 wide), 88 winning at Indiana in June, and 88 in an OC 100k here last fall. He has three 88s in his last five and is 3-0-1-1 over this course. Rodriguez (18%) and Lobo (25% at the meet) are 19% together. He is a deep closer (late 101) who has not run since September, and a 6-year-old with one win in his last ten. About 15%; fair 5.7-1, and 6-1 gives 1.05. Fair. Bet at 7-1 or better.

**War At Sea (15-1)** is the value. He ran 93 two back in an OC 100k N2X turf mile at Del Mar, 4th and beaten under three lengths after angling out, and 88 before that when 3rd. Throw out the last line: a 78 on dirt eight days later, a surface try on short rest. He has a 95 life best at this distance and is 11-2-1-1 at a mile on turf. Torres is 22% at the meet, and new trainer DiVito (12% this year, 14% first off the claim) is 20% with him over 10 starts. The risk: he is a 7-year-old, 0-for-5 this year, ten weeks off, with a new barn and no start on this course. About 9%; fair 10-1, and 15-1 gives 1.44. Overlay.

**Restore (15-1, AE)** is the improver. His figures go 51, 75, 84, and the 84 came winning a 1 1/16 N1X on this turf at 22-1 in September, leading all the way and holding gamely. He is 3-for-5 this year. Gutierrez (10%) and Stewart (19% this year) are 6-for-19 together at Churchill (32%, $5.73 ROI), the best combo in the race. The questions are the class step to N$Y company, cutting back to a mile with a lot of speed inside him, and getting in off the AE list. About 8%; fair 11.5-1, and 15-1 gives 1.28. Overlay, just.

**Sushi Man (12-1)** ran 83 in a 1 1/8 N2X at Keeneland (3rd, outkicked) after 85 winning a Canterbury stakes. He is 3-for-5 at the distance. Arrieta is 9% at the meet and the J/T is 0-for-8, so he stays out of the top spots. About 7%; fair 13-1, and 12-1 gives 0.91. Underlay.

## Bottom line

War At Sea is the top pick at 13-1 or better; he is the best overlay on the board. Restore is the second choice at about 15-1 or better, and only if he draws in (if he does not, Theismann takes the second spot at 6-1 or better). Maui Strong is the horse to beat but is an underlay with a cold combo. Watch the board on War At Sea: a big drift past about 25-1 is a warning.

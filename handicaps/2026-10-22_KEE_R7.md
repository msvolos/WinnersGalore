**Passionfruit (6-1)** is the top pick. Per start is about $18,200 on 4 starts, all this year. The trend is the best in the field: Beyers 68, 56, 74, 80. The knock is the connections: Sheehy is 0 for 27 at the meet and Eurton 0 for 3, though the pair are 17% together with a $2.51 ROI. That weak barn is part of why the price is fair.

**Lobby Rendzvous (12-1)** and **Delicious Diva (20-1)**. Top figures of 77 and 71, chronic N1X losers, behind Passionfruit last out. Toss.

## Bottom line

Passionfruit at 6-1 is the play: improving, proven at the trip and class, at a fair price. Athenix at 10-1 is the second choice on the rising dirt figures and the bullet work, and Ivory and Ebony at 8-1 is a small saver on figures. Morning line odds; check the board at post time.

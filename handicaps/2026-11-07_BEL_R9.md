## The contenders

**Bodacious Bay (5-2)** - the top pick and the horse to beat. Beyers 69, 83, 84, 91, 93 on a line that has gone up every start. The 91 was a game 2nd in the Monmouth Oaks (G3), and the 93 came winning the Cotillion (G1) at Parx Sept 19 by 3 1/2 lengths over a field that included Tap to Open and Iron Orchard. Her 93 is the best figure in the race by 4 points. She is 3-1-2-0 on wet tracks, including a clear maiden win on slop at Churchill, and 3-2-1-0 at the distance. Per-start is $141.7k, by far the best in the field. Joseph/Husbands are 24% together over 418 starts. The flags: Husbands has had only 1 mount at the meet, Joseph is 2 for 22 here (9%), and she comes off a career top 7 weeks ago, so a step back is possible. She stalks (TimeformUS early 109), which suits a race with several speed horses. About 35% to win; fair odds 1.9-1, and 5-2 gives a value ratio of 1.23. Fair, just short of an overlay.

She was 4th in the Cotillion, 6 wide and gaining, behind Bodacious Bay. Cox is 28% at the meet, Franco 21%, and the pair are 5 for 10 at Belmont (28% overall). Her figures top out around 85, so she needs a career best to beat the top pick, and she has the most early speed in the race (TimeformUS early 114), which may mean a fight up front with Iron Orchard and Cognition. About 16%; fair 5.3-1, and 5-1 gives 0.96.

**Intrepids Fantasy (8-1)** - the unbeaten Delaware filly. 3-for-3 with Beyers 60, 83, 87, the 87 an easy win in an OC 50k on a muddy track at Delaware Sept 3. She is 2-for-2 on wet tracks. **Answered Prayers (8-1)** - the honest one. Beyers 76, 75, 78, 79, 84, with the 84 a clear 2nd in a stakes at Churchill. She is 4-1-3-0 at the distance and keeps hitting the board. ## Bottom line

Neither pick is an overlay on the morning line. Bodacious Bay is the top pick and becomes a bet at 13-5 (2.6-1) or better. The morning line of 5-2 is just under that, so take her if the board drifts a tick. Tap to Open is the second choice and needs about 7-1 to be a bet. If Bodacious Bay goes off below 2-1, she is the horse to beat and no bet; if Tap to Open is 7-1 or better at post, bet her instead. The risk with the top pick is a step back after a career-best G1 win with a rider new to the meet.

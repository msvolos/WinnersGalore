## Bottom line

Define at 9-2 is the play: rising figures, a last-out 86 that matches the favorite, and the best rider and trainer combination at the meet. Dakotah Blue at 10-1 is the second choice and the value. She already won a graded mile on turf, and she has a hot new barn. Essential Lady at 8-1 is the third horse to use. Watch for That's How We Love to scratch. If Define drifts under 7-2 on the board, she falls off your list and Dakotah Blue becomes the bet.

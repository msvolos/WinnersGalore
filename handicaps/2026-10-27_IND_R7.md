**1. Never Really Know (Ire) (8-1), top pick.** The mile is her trip: 4-2-0-2 at the distance and 2-1-0-1 on this course. Her turf figures are climbing and sitting at the top of the playable group: 66, 72 (won Sep 3 here, rallied 2-wide and lasted), 72 (3rd Sep 28 at this exact mile in an OC20k as the 2-1 favorite, finished well on the rail). I am throwing out the 35 at Churchill in April (weakened badly, wide) and the 52 on dirt at Fair Grounds (wrong surface). Last year's per-start ($18.8k on two starts) shows she was a useful filly early, and the Irish pedigree (No Nay Never) fits firm turf. Norm Casse wins at 16% on turf with a $2.27 ROI and Murrill rides at 16% at the meet. Stalking style should get a tracking trip behind a contested pace. The risk is the class step from claiming and OC20k company into an open N1X allowance, and her 72 still needs a few points to reach par 78.

## Bottom line

Never Really Know at 8-1 is the play: proven at the mile and the course, rising turf figures, fair barn. Cinquante Cinq is the second choice and the one to key with her, provided she holds 7-2 or better on the board. Sudden Rise is a small flyer at 8-1 for the late kick.

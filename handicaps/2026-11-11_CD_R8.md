## The contenders

**Sinister Smile (5-1)** is the top pick. His one turf try was an 82 at Kentucky Downs (6 1/2f, swung widest and gaining, 4th beaten about a length), and last out he was 2nd with a 79 in the Keeneland N1X that came off the turf onto a sloppy track. His life best is an 85, and that came winning at a mile. The real edge is the barn: Irad Ortiz Jr. is 37% at the meet, Joe Sharp is 27% at the meet, and together they are 50% at Churchill ($2.84 ROI). The risk is plain: he is 1-for-17 with five seconds, so he has a habit of finding one horse better, and this is his first turf route. About 20%; fair 4-1, and 5-1 gives 1.20.

Underlay, but the most likely winner along with the top pick.

## Bottom line

Nobody is an overlay on the morning line. Top pick is **Sinister Smile** (hot Ortiz/Sharp barn, 82 in his only turf start, 85 life best at a mile); he becomes a bet at 21-4 or better. Second choice is **Golden Channel** (best recent turf figures, 4-1-1-0 at the distance); he needs 9-1 or better. Irish Law is the horse to beat on speed and becomes the value at 6-1 or longer.
